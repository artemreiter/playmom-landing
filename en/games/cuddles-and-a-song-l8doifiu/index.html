Cuddles and a song

15-18 monthsemotionsno prepno messbedtime6 min

Rock together in a cuddle to a quiet song and pause so your baby can show whether they want more.

Setup

No preparation needed. Sit on the sofa or the floor and invite your baby to sit on your lap.

Steps

  1. Hold your baby close and rock slowly from side to side, softly singing a familiar song.
  2. Stop the song and freeze. Wait to see if your baby shows with a movement or a sound that they want more.
  3. Carry on when your baby asks. Let them decide: keep rocking or climb down and play.

Safety

Keep your baby’s face free: don’t press it against your chest or shoulder. Rock gently, with no bouncing or tossing.

Let go as soon as your baby starts to wriggle out.

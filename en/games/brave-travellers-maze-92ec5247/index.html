Brave traveller’s maze

12-15 monthsgross motor skillsno mess17 min

Lay out a cushion, a rolled-up duvet and a box cave on the floor, and your baby crawls along the route to a toy.

Setup

Lay a sofa cushion, a rolled-up duvet and a large box on its side, open at both ends, on the floor.

Steps

  1. Crawl the route yourself first: over the cushion, over the roll and through the box.
  2. Sit a toy at the exit of the box and call your baby: “Crawl over to it.”
  3. Steady your baby on the cushion and the roll, and wait on the other side of the box.
  4. Let your baby move the toy to any obstacle, and now it’s your turn to crawl.

What you need

  • a sofa cushion
  • a duvet
  • a large cardboard box
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Don’t build a tunnel from chairs or stand anything upright: everything lies on the floor.

Use a box with no staples or sharp edges; stay within arm’s reach.

Happy dance

15-18 monthsemotionsno prepno mess8 min

Dance to a favourite song, copy your baby’s moves and freeze together when the music stops.

Setup

Nothing to prepare. Find your baby’s favourite song on your phone and clear some space.

Steps

  1. Put the song on and dance along with a big smile: “Let’s dance.”
  2. Show one move: clapping, stamping or swaying. Wait to see if your baby copies it.
  3. Copy any move your baby makes, as if it were a new dance.
  4. Pause the music and freeze together. Start it again and ask: “More?”
  5. Let your baby show with a gesture whether to keep dancing or finish with a hug.

What you need

  • a phone or speaker with music

Safety

Don’t spin your baby round or lift them by the arms: dance beside them or hold them close in your arms.

Keep the music quiet and clear small toys off the floor.

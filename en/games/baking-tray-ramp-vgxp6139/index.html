Baking tray ramp

12-15 monthsgross motor skillsno mess17 min

Build a ramp from a baking tray and send a ball, a block and a paper roll down it, checking together what rolls and what doesn’t.

Steps

  1. Put the ball at the top of the ramp: “One, two, three, let go.” The ball rolls down into the cushion.

What you need

  • a baking tray or a large chopping board
  • 3 books
  • a ball
  • a block
  • a toilet paper roll
  • a sofa cushion

Safety

Hold the ramp steady with your hand so it can’t slide onto your baby.

The ball and block are bigger than your baby’s fist and have no small parts.

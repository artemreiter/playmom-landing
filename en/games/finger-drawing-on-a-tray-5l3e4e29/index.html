Finger drawing on a tray

18-21 monthsattention20 min

Draw with your fingers in a thick mix of cornstarch and water on a dark tray, wipe it smooth and start again.

Setup

Mix 4 spoons of cornstarch with 2 spoons of water in a bowl and pour a thin layer onto a dark tray.

Steps

  1. Run a finger through the mix: a dark trail appears. Draw a circle: “The sun.”
  2. Let your child move their fingers and palms through it. Name the marks: “A palm print. A line.”
  3. Offer a choice: tap the finger for rain, or draw a wavy snake.
  4. Smooth the mix flat with your palm and the drawing disappears. Ask who draws next.

What you need

  • a dark tray
  • 4 tablespoons of cornstarch
  • water
  • a bowl
  • a towel

Safety

Use only water and cornstarch your child has already eaten without an allergy: no foam, cream, soap or dyes.

Keep the bowl to yourself; if your child puts their hands in their mouth, wipe them and end the game.

Sofa Everest

12-15 monthsgross motor skillsno mess13 min

Your baby climbs onto the sofa to reach teddy, clambers over the bolster and slides down feet first onto cushions.

Setup

Lay 2-3 large cushions on the floor in front of the sofa. Put a favourite toy on the sofa seat.

Steps

  1. Show the toy: “Teddy’s up the mountain. Can you reach him?” Let your baby climb while you stay close to spot them.
  2. Put a bolster or rolled-up duvet on the seat: let your baby clamber over it to teddy.
  3. Show how to get down: turn your baby onto their tummy, feet to the edge, and help them slide onto the cushions.
  4. Let your baby choose where teddy waits next time, behind the bolster or at the edge, and climb up again.

What you need

  • a sofa
  • 2-3 large cushions
  • a bolster or duvet
  • a favourite toy

Safety

While your baby is on the sofa, keep your hands close and don’t let them stand on the back or arms.

Your baby only comes down feet first onto the cushions; don’t move the sofa.

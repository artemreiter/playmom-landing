Tickles and giggles

12-15 monthsemotionsno prepno mess3 min

Creep your fingers slowly towards your baby, pause and tickle lightly, as long as your baby finds it funny.

Setup

Settle down with your baby on the sofa or the floor, facing each other.

Steps

  1. Say “I’m going to get you” and slowly move your fingers towards your baby’s tummy.
  2. Pause right by the tummy, then tickle lightly: “Tickle, tickle.”
  3. Stop and ask: “More?” Carry on only if your baby reaches out, laughs or offers their tummy.
  4. Hold out your palm: let your baby tickle you, and laugh out loud.

Safety

Tickle lightly and briefly.

Stop right away if your baby turns away, pushes your hand off or fusses.

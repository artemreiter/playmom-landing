Magnifying glass expedition

3-4 yearsindependence35 min

Collect leaves, pebbles and sticks on a walk or on the balcony, examine them through a magnifying glass, and set up a mini museum at home.

Setup

Pack a kid’s magnifying glass, a notebook, a pencil and a small box for finds into a bag.

Steps

  1. On your walk or balcony, suggest finding 3 leaves, 2 pebbles and a stick, and let your child decide where to start.
  2. Look at the finds through the magnifying glass and ask which one is smooth, which is rough, which is heavy.
  3. At home, your child sorts the finds into piles and draws where each one was lying, while you label them.
  4. Let your child give the family a tour of their museum.

What you need

  • a kid’s magnifying glass
  • a notebook and pencil
  • a small box for finds
  • a bag

Safety

Pick things up only from the ground, skip berries, mushrooms, feathers and litter, and wash hands after the walk.

Never point the magnifying glass at the sun, and don’t let your child climb on balcony boxes near the railing.

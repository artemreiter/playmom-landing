Shadow animal ball

3-4 yearsmovementno mess15 min

Your child stands in front of the light and makes a bear, a hare and a bird as shadows on the wall while you guess the animals.

Setup

Dim the lights but keep the floor visible. Point a torch with a screwed-down battery cover at a light-coloured wall.

Steps

  1. Your child stands between the torch and the wall. The bear waddles along: let your child show it as a shadow.
  2. Let your child choose the next animal: the bunny hops on the spot, the bird flaps its wings.
  3. Riddle time: your child silently shows an animal as a shadow and you guess. Then it’s your turn, with the shadow of your free hand.
  4. Let your child step back towards the torch and return to the wall: the shadow grows and shrinks.

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a light-coloured wall

Safety

The torch stays in an adult’s hand the whole time, pointing at the wall, not into anyone’s eyes.

No running in the half dark: jumping on the spot only.

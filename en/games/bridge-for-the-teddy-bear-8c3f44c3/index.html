Bridge for the teddy bear

30-36 monthsindependenceno mess25 min

Your child builds a bridge from tubes, boxes and magazines across a towel river so the teddy bear can reach the other side.

Setup

Lay a towel river on the floor. Pile up tubes, 2 to 3 boxes without staples and old magazines nearby, and sit the teddy bear on the shore.

Steps

  1. Ask: “How can teddy cross the river without getting his paws wet?”
  2. Let your child try on their own: laying magazines, setting up boxes, joining tubes. Help if they ask.
  3. When the bridge is ready, let your child walk the teddy bear across it.
  4. If the bridge falls apart, ask what to change. Then your child builds a bridge for your toy too.

What you need

  • a towel
  • paper towel tubes
  • 2 to 3 cardboard boxes without staples
  • old magazines
  • a soft toy bigger than your child's fist

Safety

No ribbons or string; use boxes without staples or sharp edges.

Keep the bridge on the floor, don't stand on it.

Portals to the stars

3-4 yearsgross motor skillsno mess19 min

Jump from portal to portal on the floor and make up which planet you land on and who you meet there.

Setup

Stick 4 cushion-sized squares of tape on the floor, a big step apart: these are the portals.

Steps

  1. Name the first portal: “This is the gate to the Moon,” and jump into it on both feet with a “whoosh.”
  2. Let your child jump from portal to portal, telling you at each one who they met on that planet.
  3. Let your child decide where the next portal leads, and choose the jump: both feet, or a big stride.
  4. Swap: your child names the planet and the kind of jump, and you do the jumping.

What you need

  • masking tape

Safety

Use only tape for the portals, because scarves and towels slide on the floor.

Jump barefoot or in non-slip socks, well away from furniture corners.

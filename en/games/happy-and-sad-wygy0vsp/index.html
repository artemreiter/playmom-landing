Happy and sad

21-24 monthsemotionsno prep10 min

Draw two faces on a sheet, one happy and one sad: you draw the mouth, and your child adds dot eyes to each.

Setup

Put a sheet of paper and a felt-tip pen on the table.

Steps

  1. Draw a circle with a smile and say: “This is a face, it’s happy.”
  2. Give your child the felt-tip pen: let them put dot eyes on the face.
  3. Draw a second circle with a downturned mouth: “And this face is sad.” Let your child add dot eyes to it too.
  4. Let your child choose which face to draw next, happy or sad, and draw it together.

What you need

  • paper
  • a felt-tip pen

Safety

Keep the felt-tip pen with you between rounds and take it away if your child puts it in their mouth.

If the sad face upsets your child, show the happy one and end the game.

Pouring through a funnel

24-30 monthsindependence20 min

Your child pours water through a funnel into a bottle, stops at the tape line and tells you when to pour.

Setup

Put a tray with raised edges on a towel. On it, place two plastic cups with a little water in each, a bottle without its lid and a funnel. Stick a strip of tape on the bottle.

Steps

  1. Put the funnel in the bottle and slowly pour in some water: “In it goes.”
  2. Give your child a cup. Let them pour by themselves: any misses stay on the tray.
  3. Point to the strip: “Shall we fill it up to the line?” Let your child stop when the water reaches it.
  4. Swap: your child says “pour” and “stop”, and you do the pouring.

What you need

  • a tray with raised edges
  • a towel
  • 2 plastic cups
  • a clear plastic bottle
  • a funnel
  • masking tape

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach. Keep the water shallow, clean and with no colouring.

Wipe up spills on the floor straight away and put the bottle lid away.

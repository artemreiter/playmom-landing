Cooking for creatures

3-5 yearsindependence28 min

Your child pretends to cook potions and soups for a dragon or a fairy from pasta, leaves and water, and you taste them for the creature.

Setup

Lay down a wipe-clean tablecloth and set out two bowls, a spoon, a glass with a little water in the bottom, dry pasta and leaves.

Steps

  1. Ask your child who the kitchen is cooking for today: a dragon, a fairy or a gnome.
  2. Your child adds whatever they like to a bowl, stirs it and adds water drop by drop.
  3. Ask what the dish is called and what it can do: “Is it a bravery soup?”
  4. Pretend to taste the dish for the creature, then swap: your child orders and you cook.

What you need

  • 2 bowls
  • a spoon
  • a glass of water
  • dry pasta
  • leaves
  • a wipe-clean tablecloth

Safety

It’s all pretend: make sure nothing from the magic kitchen ends up in a mouth.

No beads, buttons, thread or spices; while there’s water on the table, stay within arm’s reach.

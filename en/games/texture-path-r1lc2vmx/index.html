Texture path

12-15 monthsgross motor skills17 min

Walk barefoot hand in hand with your baby over a cushion, a mat, a blanket and a damp towel to their favourite toy.

Setup

Lay out a flat cushion, a yoga mat, a folded blanket and a slightly damp towel in a line on the rug. Put the toy at the end.

Steps

  1. Take off your baby’s socks and hold their hand at the first station.
  2. Step onto the cushion: “Soft.” Let your baby stamp about.
  3. Next the mat: “Hard”, the blanket: “Fluffy”, the towel: “Cool”. Name each feeling.
  4. At the end your baby picks up the toy. On the next lap let them choose which station to start from.

What you need

  • a flat cushion
  • a yoga mat
  • a blanket
  • a towel
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Hold your baby’s hand all the way; everything lies on the rug and doesn’t slip.

The towel is only damp, with no puddles. Dry your baby’s feet after it.

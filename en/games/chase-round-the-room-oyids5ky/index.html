Chase round the room

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno prepno mess9 min

Slowly chase your baby round the room, catch them in a hug, then swap: now they chase you.

Setup

No preparation needed. Play where there are no cables or scattered toys on the floor.

Steps

  1. Say: “I’m going to catch you,” and move slowly towards your baby on all fours or on foot.
  2. When your baby runs, follow with pauses so they have time to look back.
  3. Catch them, give a gentle hug and ask: “Again?” Keep going if your baby laughs and runs off again.
  4. Swap: say “Oh, I’m running away” and slowly move off while your baby chases you.

Safety

Run on a level floor, away from stairs and sharp furniture corners.

Move slowly and stop if your baby is scared or tired.

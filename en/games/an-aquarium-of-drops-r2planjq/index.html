An aquarium of drops

24-30 monthsfine motor skills19 min

Draw fish on kitchen roll with felt-tip pens, and let your toddler drip clean water from a spoon onto them so the fish blur and swim.

Setup

Draw three fish on a sheet of kitchen roll with washable felt-tip pens, lay it on a tray, and pour a little water into the bottom of a bowl.

Steps

  1. Drip water from the spoon onto a fish: “Look, the fish is swimming.”
  2. Give your toddler the spoon so they can scoop water themselves and drip it onto any fish.
  3. Take turns dripping, and ask which fish to bring to life next.
  4. Draw new fish in the colours your toddler chooses, and start again.

What you need

  • kitchen roll
  • washable felt-tip pens
  • a teaspoon
  • a stable bowl
  • a tray
  • water

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach: there’s only a little water in the bowl, and the felt-tip pens stay with you.

Don’t let your toddler put the wet kitchen roll in their mouth, and wipe up any spilled water right away.

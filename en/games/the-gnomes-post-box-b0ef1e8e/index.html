The gnomes’ post box

24-30 monthsfine motor skillsno messbedtime22 min

Post letters to the gnomes by posting playing cards through slots in a box, turning each card so it fits.

Setup

With a craft knife, cut two slots a little wider than a card into the lid of a shoebox, one lengthways and one crossways, then put the knife away. Draw a little door on the box.

Steps

  1. Knock on the box: “Knock knock, letter for the gnomes,” and drop a card through a slot.
  2. Give your baby a card: let them find a slot and turn the card so it fits through.
  3. Once the letters run out, open the lid: “The gnomes got everything.”
  4. Now you’re the postie: your baby hands over the letters one by one and shows you which slot to post each one through.

What you need

  • a shoebox with a lid
  • a deck of playing cards
  • a craft knife for an adult
  • a marker

Safety

The box has no staples, and the edges of the slots are smooth, check them with a finger before playing.

Don’t use wooden sticks, and swap out any card gone soft from being chewed.

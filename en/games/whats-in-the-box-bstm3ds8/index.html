What’s in the box

9-12 monthsfine motor skillsno mess16 min

Shake a box of toys and pull one out through the gap under the lid, then your baby takes out and hides the finds themselves.

Setup

Put three toys bigger than your baby’s fist in a shoebox, such as a rattle, a ball and a block. Put the lid on, leaving a gap for a hand.

Steps

  1. Shake the box: “There’s someone in there. Can you hear?”
  2. Slip your hand through the gap, take out the rattle and give it a shake: “Found it.”
  3. Put it back and hold the box out to your baby. Let them reach through the gap or lift the lid.
  4. Name each find. Let your baby decide: take out the next one or hide the one they found back inside.

What you need

  • a shoebox with a lid
  • 3 toys bigger than your baby’s fist

Safety

Toys bigger than your baby’s fist, with no small parts; a box with no staples or sharp edges.

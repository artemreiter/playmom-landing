Muffin tin volcano

30-36 monthsthinking22 min

Your child picks a cup, and you pour in vinegar: the baking soda fizzes up into colourful foam in each one.

Setup

Set a muffin tin on a tray, spoon baking soda into each cup, and add a drop of different food colouring to each one. Pour vinegar into a cup and keep it to yourself.

Steps

  1. Let your child pick a cup and give the command: “Go.”
  2. Pour in a teaspoon of vinegar: “Fizzzz.” Watch the foam together and name the colour.
  3. Ask which cup is next, and keep going until every one has erupted.
  4. Scoop a spoonful of foam from whichever cup your child picks into the next one and name the new colour.

What you need

  • a muffin tin
  • a tray
  • baking soda
  • vinegar
  • food colouring
  • a teaspoon
  • a cup

Safety

Keep the baking soda, vinegar and food colouring to yourself: your child gives the commands and doesn’t touch or taste the foam.

If any splashes get in the eyes, rinse them with water.

Ice surprise

9-12 monthssenses20 min

Your baby touches the cold ice and pours warm water over it from a cup until the frozen toy is free.

Steps

  1. Let your baby touch the ice: “Cold, slippery.”

What you need

  • 2 plastic containers
  • 2 large bath toys
  • a tray with raised edges
  • a plastic cup of warm water

Safety

The water is warm to the touch and only at the bottom of the cup; stay within arm’s reach.

Freeze only large toys, no berries or small bits. Clear away chipped-off pieces of ice at once.

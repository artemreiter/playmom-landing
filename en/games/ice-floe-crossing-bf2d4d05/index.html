Ice floe crossing

18-21 monthsgross motor skillsno mess7 min

Lay folded towels on the rug as ice floes and cross them together, then let your baby choose the path.

Setup

Lay four folded towels on the rug, one small step apart from each other.

Steps

  1. Say that the water all around is cold, and step onto the first ice floe while holding your baby’s hand.
  2. Pause on every ice floe: “We’re standing here, staying dry.”
  3. Let your baby choose which ice floe to step onto next.
  4. Swap roles: your baby leads, and you step behind them, marvelling at every ice floe.

What you need

  • four towels
  • clear space on the rug

Safety

Keep the towels on the rug rather than a smooth floor, and play barefoot or in non-slip socks.

There’s no need to jump: hold your baby’s hand while their steps are still unsteady.

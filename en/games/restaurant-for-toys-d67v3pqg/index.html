Restaurant for toys

3-4 yearsindependence30 min

Your child opens a restaurant: takes orders from the toys, moulds the dishes out of modelling clay and serves them, then you swap roles.

Setup

Sit 3 toys around a box that’s the table, and put modelling clay, plastic lids for plates, and paper money nearby.

Steps

  1. Your child, the waiter, asks each toy what they’d like to order, while you answer for the toys.
  2. In the kitchen, your child moulds the order out of modelling clay: a red tomato, yellow cheese, a brown patty.
  3. Your child serves the dishes, and the toys say thank you and pay with the paper money.
  4. Swap roles: you’re the waiter, and your child orders dishes and pays.

What you need

  • modelling clay
  • plastic lids
  • paper money
  • a box
  • 3 soft toys

Safety

Use only paper money instead of buttons or coins, and don’t taste the modelling clay.

Wash hands after playing and put the modelling clay away.

Who lives in the box

15-18 monthsthinkingno mess15 min

A toy’s ear or tail pokes out from under the box lid: your baby guesses who’s inside and opens the box.

Setup

Take a shoebox with no staples and 3 soft toys with clear ears, tails or paws.

Steps

  1. Put the bunny in the box and cover it with the lid so an ear sticks out: “Who’s hiding in there?”
  2. Wait: your baby might touch the ear, point or make a sound. Lift the lid: “Bunny.”
  3. Hide another toy, leaving a paw or tail outside, and ask again who’s there.
  4. Swap: your baby hides a toy and you guess, sometimes getting it wrong so they can correct you.

What you need

  • a shoebox with a lid
  • 3 soft toys

Safety

The box has no staples or sharp edges; no need to cut a slot.

Use toys with no buttons or small parts.

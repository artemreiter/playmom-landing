Bubble painting

6-9 monthsattention15 min

Blow soap bubbles over dark paper: they land, pop and leave wet circles while your child reaches for them.

Setup

Sit on the floor, sit your child between your knees facing away from you, and lay a sheet of dark heavy paper in front of them.

Steps

  1. Blow a few bubbles over the sheet, away from your child: “Look, they're flying.”
  2. When a bubble lands on the paper and pops, point to the wet circle: “Pop. A circle.”
  3. Watch where your child reaches or looks, and send the next bubbles that way.
  4. If your child pats the sheet, name the marks: “There's a circle, and another.” Keep going while they're interested.

What you need

  • bubble solution with a wand
  • a sheet of dark heavy paper
  • a wet towel

Safety

Keep the solution and wand to yourself, and never blow bubbles into your child's face.

If your child puts their hands in their mouth, wipe them with a wet towel right away, and again after play.

Tunnel of light

15-18 monthsmovementno mess20 min

Your child crawls under a table draped with a blanket toward the flashlight and a favorite toy, then you crawl through too.

Setup

Drape a blanket over the dining table so both sides stay open as entrances.

Steps

  1. Sit at the far end, put your child's favorite toy next to you and shine the flashlight on the floor.
  2. Call out: “Crawl to the light.” If your child feels unsure, reach a hand into the tunnel.
  3. When your child crawls out, hug them: “You made it.” Ask if they want to crawl again.
  4. Swap: your child waits at the entrance with the toy while you crawl through, flashlight off and in your hand.

What you need

  • a dining table
  • a blanket
  • a flashlight with a screwed battery cover
  • a favorite soft toy

Safety

Use only a stable table, no chairs, both ends open; if your child pulls the blanket down, remove it.

Only you hold the flashlight with a screwed battery cover, and point it at the floor, never at eyes.

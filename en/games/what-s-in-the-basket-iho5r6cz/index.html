What's in the basket

9-12 monthsindependenceno mess10 min

Fill a low basket with big household things and let your child take them out one by one, look them over and put them back.

Setup

Put 5 things bigger than your child's fist in a low basket: a wooden spoon, a big ball, a cloth, a cup, a soft toy.

Steps

  1. Put the basket in front of your child and look inside together: “What's in there?”
  2. Let your child take things out one by one themselves. Name each one: “Spoon. Ball.”
  3. Show what can be done with each thing: tap the spoon, wave the cloth, roll the ball.
  4. When the basket is empty, put one thing back and wait: your child will take it out again or add their own.

What you need

  • a low basket
  • 5 things bigger than your child's fist

Safety

Use only clean, whole things bigger than your child's fist, no small parts, strings or glass.

Stay close the whole time: your child tries things with their mouth.

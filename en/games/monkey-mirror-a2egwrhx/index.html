Monkey mirror

15-18 monthsemotionsno prepno mess6 min

Take turns pulling funny faces: your baby copies yours, then you copy theirs exactly.

Setup

Nothing to prepare. Sit facing your baby, an arm’s length away.

Steps

  1. Say: “I’m a monkey.” Stick out your tongue and wait a couple of seconds to see what your baby does.
  2. Puff out your cheeks and tap them gently: “Pff.” Then open your mouth wide: “Aaah.”
  3. Cover your own eyes with your hands and uncover them: “Peekaboo.”
  4. Now it’s your baby’s turn. Copy any face or sound they make exactly.
  5. Repeat the face your baby laughed at most, or wait for a new one from them.

Safety

Cover only your own eyes with your hands; don’t touch your baby’s face.

If your baby turns away or frowns, take a break or stop.

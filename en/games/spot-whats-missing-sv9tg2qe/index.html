Spot what’s missing

18-21 monthsthinkingno prepno messbedtime8 min

Cover a few things with a towel and remove one, then let your baby spot what’s missing, and later hide an item themselves.

Setup

No preparation needed. Grab a towel and 2 familiar things bigger than your baby’s fist, for example a ball and a cup.

Steps

  1. Line the things up in front of your baby, name each one, and let them touch each item.
  2. Cover them with the towel, slip one item behind your back underneath it, and lift the towel away.
  3. Ask: “What’s missing?” If it’s too tricky, bring the item back out: “There it is, the ball.”
  4. Swap roles: your baby hides an item, and you guess. If it’s going well, add a third item.

What you need

  • a towel
  • 2 to 3 familiar things

Safety

Use things bigger than your baby’s fist with no small parts.

Cover only the objects with the towel, never your baby.

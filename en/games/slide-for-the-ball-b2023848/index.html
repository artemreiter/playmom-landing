Slide for the ball

18-21 monthsthinkingno mess13 min

Build a slide from a tray and a cushion: your child launches a ball, watches it roll into a basket and picks who goes next.

Steps

  1. Put the ball at the top of the slide and let go: “Off it goes.” Show how it rolls into the basket.

What you need

  • a large plastic tray
  • a sofa cushion
  • 2 balls bigger than your child's fist
  • a basket

Safety

Use only balls bigger than your child's fist, no cotton balls or small balls.

Keep the slide on the floor, don't climb on it; hold the tray steady if it slips.

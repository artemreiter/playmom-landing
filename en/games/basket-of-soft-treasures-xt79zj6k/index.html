Basket of soft treasures

6-9 monthsmovementno mess11 min

Fill a low basket with soft toys and take turns with your baby pulling them out, hiding them and finding them again.

Steps

  1. Take out one toy, show your baby and say: “Here’s a treasure.”

2 games free, then by subscription with a trial period

Open in the app

What you need

  • a low basket or tub
  • 3 soft toys
  • a fabric ball

Safety

The toys are bigger than your baby’s fist, with no buttons or small parts, and the basket has no sharp edges or staples.

Don’t put scarves or blankets in the basket, so the fabric can’t cover your baby’s face.

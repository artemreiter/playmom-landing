Surprise bundle

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno mess12 min

Wrap a toy in a tea towel and let your baby pull the fabric to find what’s jingling inside.

Steps

  1. Shake the bundle with the rattle: “What’s jingling in there?”

What you need

  • two tea towels
  • a rattle
  • a soft block

Safety

Use toys bigger than your baby’s fist, with no small parts or sharp edges.

Don’t cover your baby’s face with the fabric, and put the tea towels away after playing.

Giant’s obstacle course

15-18 monthsmovementno mess18 min

Work through a mini obstacle course together: step over a log, crawl under you, climb over cushions, and throw a ball.

Setup

Lay a rolled-up towel, three sofa cushions and a box with a ball in a row on the floor.

Steps

  1. Holding your child’s hand, step over the towel log: “Lift that leg high, you’re a giant.”
  2. Get down on all fours and let your child crawl underneath you like a mouse.
  3. Let your child cross the cushions however they like, crawling or walking, while holding your hand.
  4. At the finish, your child throws the ball, and they decide which obstacle starts the next round.

What you need

  • a big towel
  • three sofa cushions
  • a box
  • a ball bigger than your child’s fist

Safety

While your child crawls underneath you, stay still and don’t lower yourself onto them.

No ropes, ribbons or chairs are needed, and the ball is bigger than your child’s fist.

Clothes peg sun

18-21 monthsemotionsno mess37 min

Clip coloured pegs round a cardboard circle as rays, and your baby takes them off and helps put them back.

Steps

  1. Show your baby the sun and take off one peg, saying its colour.

What you need

  • 6 coloured clothes pegs
  • a cardboard box
  • scissors
  • a bowl

Safety

Use large pegs with a stiff spring, and check the spring doesn’t come out.

Don’t clip the pegs onto your baby’s fingers, clothes or ears.

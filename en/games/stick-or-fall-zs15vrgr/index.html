Stick or fall

15-18 monthsthinkingno mess17 min

Press different things onto sticky tape on a door and find out together which ones stick and which ones fall.

Setup

Stick a strip of masking tape to a door at your child’s chest height, sticky side out, with the ends taped down. Put a napkin, a sock, a lid and a wooden spoon nearby.

Steps

  1. Press the napkin onto the tape: “It stuck.” Then the spoon, and let go: “It fell.”
  2. Let your child pick the next thing and press it on themselves. Say what happened.
  3. Let your child peel off what stuck and decide what to test again.
  4. Swap: your child hands you something, and you press it onto the tape.

What you need

  • masking tape
  • a paper napkin
  • a sock
  • a large plastic tub lid
  • a wooden spoon

Safety

Use only things too big to fit in the mouth. If your child puts the tape in their mouth, take the strip down.

Don’t use magnets: even a large fridge magnet can break into small pieces.

Catch the flying sparks

12-15 monthsmovementno mess8 min

Toss light scarves in the air, your baby catches the slowly falling sparks, then tosses them back to you.

Steps

  1. Toss a scarf up above you: “A spark is flying.” Let your baby watch it slowly float down.

What you need

  • 3-4 light scarves

Safety

Toss the scarves in front of your baby, not above them, so the fabric doesn’t land on their face or head.

Use whole scarves with no wadding or small scraps, and put them away after playing.

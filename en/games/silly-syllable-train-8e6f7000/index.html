Silly syllable train

18-21 monthsspeechno mess14 min

Sit toy animals in cushion carriages and ride together, saying “choo-choo,” “toot-toot” and the animals’ sounds.

Setup

Lay 3 sofa cushions in a row on the rug: these are the carriages. Sit 3 soft toys next to them.

Steps

  1. Let your baby choose the passengers and sit each one in its own carriage.
  2. Sit behind the train with your baby and rock gently: “Choo-choo, toot-toot.” Wait for your baby to say “toot-toot.”
  3. Say “Station” and ask who is getting off. Your baby points to a toy, and you make its sound: “Meow,” “Woof,” “Moo.”
  4. Wave the passenger off with “Bye-bye” and ride on. Once everyone has got off, your baby seats the passengers again.

What you need

  • 3 sofa cushions
  • 3 soft toys

Safety

Don’t tie the carriages together with string: the cushions just sit side by side.

The passengers are soft, bigger than your baby’s fist, and have no small parts.

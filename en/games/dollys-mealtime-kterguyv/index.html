Dolly’s mealtime

15-18 monthsemotionsno mess11 min

Pretend to feed a doll from a plate, then your baby takes the spoon, feeds it and chooses who to feed next.

Steps

  1. Say: “Dolly’s hungry.” Scoop up some pretend food and bring the spoon to the doll: “Yum, yum.”

Catch the runaway shadow

21-24 monthsmovementno mess10 min

Move a soft toy above the floor while your child chases its shadow and tries to step on it or slap it.

Setup

Play during the day by a sunny window or under the ceiling light. Clear the floor and take a soft toy.

Steps

  1. Hold the toy above the floor so its shadow appears next to your child: “Look, a shadow.”
  2. Slowly move the shadow across the floor. Let your child catch up and step on it or slap it with a palm.
  3. When your child catches the shadow, freeze: “Got it.” Then the shadow runs away again.
  4. Give the toy to your child: now they move the shadow and you try to catch it.

What you need

  • a soft toy
  • a sunny window or ceiling light

Safety

Play in the light on a clear floor, with no dark room and no torch.

Move the shadow slowly and away from furniture corners so your child doesn’t pick up speed.

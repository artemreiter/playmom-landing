A slope for the ball

6-9 monthsthinkingno mess11 min

Send a ball and a block down a low slope: the ball rolls, the block stays put, and your baby picks what goes next.

Setup

Rest one end of a chopping board on two books to make a low slope. Lay a towel at the bottom.

Steps

  1. Put the ball at the top of the slope and let go: “Whoosh.” Show where it stopped.
  2. Put the block on the slope: it doesn’t move. Nudge it with your finger: “Bump.”
  3. Show the ball and the block. Send down whichever one your baby reaches for, or let them push it with their hand.

What you need

  • a chopping board
  • two books
  • a towel
  • a soft ball bigger than your baby’s fist
  • a large block

Safety

The ball and block are bigger than your baby’s fist, with no strings, bells or lids.

Your baby sits with support or lies on their tummy; the board is low and can’t fall on them.

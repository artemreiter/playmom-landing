Singing funnel

9-12 monthsspeechno mess8 min

Talk and sing through a kitchen funnel while your baby marvels at the unusual sound and babbles back.

Steps

  1. Hold the wide end to your lips and softly draw out: “Aaah.” Wait for your baby to turn towards the sound.

What you need

Safety

Speak softly into the funnel and don’t hold it to your baby’s ear.

Don’t press the funnel against the face and don’t attach ribbons or bits of paper to it.

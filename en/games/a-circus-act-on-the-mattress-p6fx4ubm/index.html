A circus act on the mattress

5-6 yearsgross motor skillsno mess25 min

Your child picks circus tricks to do on the mattress, strings them into an act and performs for you, then you perform their act.

Steps

  1. Show three tricks: a log roll, a rocking seat hugging the knees, and a boat pose on the tummy.

What you need

  • a mattress or a thick duvet
  • 2 by 2 metres of clear floor space

Safety

No forward rolls over the head, shoulder stands or bridges, since they strain the neck and back.

Only one performer on the mattress at a time; stop if your child gets tired or complains of pain.

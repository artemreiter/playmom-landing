Vanishing ball trick

9-12 monthsattentionno mess13 min

Drop a soft ball into a box tunnel and wait together with your baby for it to roll out the other side.

Steps

  1. Sit facing your baby, lift one end of the box, and set a bowl at the other end.

What you need

  • a shoebox with a lid
  • two soft balls bigger than your baby’s fist
  • a bowl
  • tape
  • scissors for the adult

Safety

The balls are soft and bigger than your baby’s fist, with no pompoms or small beads.

Use a box with no staples or sharp edges, and hold it in your hand rather than attaching it to a chair.

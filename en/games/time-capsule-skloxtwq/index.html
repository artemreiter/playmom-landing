Time capsule

5-8 yearsindependenceno mess28 min

Pack a container with a drawing, a note and three things your child treasures, seal it, and set an opening date six months away.

Setup

Put a plastic container with a lid, paper, pencils, tape and a marker on the table.

Steps

  1. Suggest your child put together a message to their future self, one you’ll open together in six months.
  2. Your child draws a picture of themselves, and you write their favourite food and best friend on the back as they dictate.
  3. Your child picks three small things that matter to them and tells you why each one.
  4. Seal the container with tape and write the opening date on it, and let your child choose where to keep it.

What you need

  • a plastic container with a lid
  • paper and pencils
  • tape
  • a marker
  • three small things that belong to your child

Safety

Don’t put any food in the capsule; if there are children under three at home, store it up high.

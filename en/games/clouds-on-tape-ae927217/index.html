Clouds on tape

18-30 monthsfine motor skillsno mess13 min

Peel little clouds of scrunched-up tissue off tape on a tray together and decide where to send them.

Steps

  1. Peel off one cloud with a little “pop” and toss it up in the air.

What you need

  • a tray
  • masking tape
  • paper tissues
  • a bowl

Safety

Use only whole scrunched-up tissues bigger than a fist, no cotton wool or pompoms.

If your baby pulls a tissue or the tape towards their mouth, take it away and end the game.

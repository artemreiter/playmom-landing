Cushion mountain climber

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno mess17 min

Build a low, soft mountain on the floor, and your baby climbs up to a toy at the top and slides down onto the duvet.

Setup

Spread a thick duvet on the floor away from furniture. Put two sofa cushions and a rolled-up duvet on top: the mountain is no higher than your baby’s knee.

Steps

  1. Climb the mountain yourself: “I’m at the top.” Sit your baby’s favourite toy on the summit.
  2. Call your baby to the toy and support them at the sides on the first climb.
  3. At the top, help your baby turn round and slide down on their bottom: “Whoosh.”
  4. Let your baby put the toy wherever they like, and now you crawl after it.

What you need

  • 2 sofa cushions
  • 2 duvets
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach while your baby is on the mountain.

Don’t lean cushions or a mattress against the sofa or a wall, so nothing can fall on your baby.

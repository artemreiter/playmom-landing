Spiderweb toy rescue

3-5 yearsmovementno mess23 min

Stick a spiderweb of masking tape across a doorway, and let your child make their way through it to rescue a captured toy.

Setup

Stick strips of masking tape across the doorway in a crisscross, no higher than your child’s waist, and sit a toy behind them.

Steps

  1. Say a sleepy spider caught the toy, and it needs rescuing without waking him up.
  2. Let your child make their way through the web by crawling or stepping over the strips, whichever way they choose.
  3. If your child brushes a strip, say sleepily “Who’s there?”, freeze together, then carry on.
  4. Swap roles: your child sticks up their own web, and you make your way through it.

What you need

  • masking tape
  • a favourite soft toy

Safety

Use only masking tape across the doorway, no ropes, ribbons or chairs, and peel off any strip that comes loose right away.

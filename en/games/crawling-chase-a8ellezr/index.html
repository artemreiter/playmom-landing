Crawling chase

12-15 monthsmovementno prepno mess6 min

Crawl after your baby on all fours, catch them gently, then crawl away yourself and let them catch you.

Steps

  1. Get down on all fours next to your baby: “I’m going to catch you.”

What you need

Safety

Hug gently, tickle only a little and stop if your baby turns away or cries.

Don’t chase your baby into a corner or towards the stairs.

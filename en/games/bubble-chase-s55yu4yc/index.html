Bubble chase

9-12 monthsgross motor skills7 min

Blow bubbles low over a blanket, and your baby crawls after them and pops them with their hand.

Steps

  1. Blow a few bubbles low in front of your baby: “Look, bubbles.”

What you need

  • a tub of bubbles
  • a blanket
  • a damp cloth

Safety

Keep the tub and wand with you and close the tub after each round: your baby only catches bubbles.

If your baby rubs their eyes with a soapy hand, wipe their hands with a damp cloth; after playing, wipe their hands and the blanket.

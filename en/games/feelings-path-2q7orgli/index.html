Feelings path

24-30 monthsemotionsno mess20 min

Walk along a path of cushions to a hidden toy, showing a new feeling on your face at every stop.

Setup

Lay 4 cushions in a path on the rug and hide a toy behind the last one.

Steps

  1. At the first cushion, show happiness on your face: “This is happy, can you do it?”
  2. When your child copies you, step forward and show surprise: mouth open, eyebrows up.
  3. On the third cushion show sadness, on the fourth an angry face, naming the feeling each time.
  4. Find the treasure, then swap: your child makes the faces, and you copy and guess.

What you need

  • 4 sofa cushions
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Step from cushion to cushion rather than jumping, and lay them on the rug so they don’t slip.

Make the treasure a toy, not a sweet.

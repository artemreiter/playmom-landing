A potion of words

18-21 monthsspeechno mess14 min

Your toddler drops toys into a saucepan, you name each one and say a magic word, then you stir the potion together.

Setup

Put an empty saucepan on the floor, and lay a ladle, a ball, a large block, a soft toy and a plastic cup beside it.

Steps

  1. Name the ball and drop it into the saucepan: “Ball in the potion. Bloop bloop.”
  2. Let your toddler choose the next object and drop it in themselves, then name it and say a magic word.
  3. Stir the potion with the ladle, going “Shh shh,” and let your toddler stir too.
  4. Pretend to taste the potion and say in a squeaky voice: “Ooh, how funny.”

What you need

  • a large saucepan
  • a ladle
  • a ball
  • a large block
  • a soft toy
  • a plastic cup

Safety

Use only objects bigger than your toddler’s fist, with no pine cones, buttons or thread; keep the saucepan empty and cold.

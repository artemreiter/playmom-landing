Treasure on the seabed

2-4 yearsattentionno mess16 min

Hide large objects under a blanket, and your child feels around for the treasure you name while you count to ten.

Setup

Lay five objects bigger than your child’s fist on the floor: a ball, a block, a spoon, a sock and a toy. Cover them with a blanket: that’s the sea.

Steps

  1. Ripple the edge of the blanket: “Listen to the waves.” Name a treasure: “Find the ball.”
  2. Your child searches under the blanket with their hands while you count slowly to ten. If time runs out, start the count again.
  3. When the treasure is found, set a harder one: “Find something soft” or “something long.”
  4. Swap: your child hides the objects and sets the challenge, and you search.

What you need

  • a blanket or sheet
  • 5 objects bigger than your child’s fist

Safety

Search with hands only: head and face stay above the blanket.

Use only objects bigger than your child’s fist, with no buttons, elastic bands or tiny bits.

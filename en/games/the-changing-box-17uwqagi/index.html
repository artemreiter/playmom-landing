The changing box

24-30 monthsindependence27 min

Turn a big box into a house, a car or a ship, and your child chooses what it will be and decorates it.

Setup

Lay a large box with no staples or sharp edges on its side, and put crayons, a cushion and a toy next to it.

Steps

  1. Ask your child what the box will be: a house, a car or a ship.
  2. Cut a window where your child points while they stand back.
  3. Your child decorates the box with crayons and puts the cushion and toy inside, and you are a passenger or a guest.
  4. After a few minutes, turn the box over: your child decides what it turns into now.

What you need

  • a large cardboard box
  • wax crayons
  • scissors
  • a cushion
  • a soft toy

Safety

Only you use the scissors; put them and the offcuts away as soon as the window is cut.

Don’t stack the box on another one or close the flaps while your child is inside.

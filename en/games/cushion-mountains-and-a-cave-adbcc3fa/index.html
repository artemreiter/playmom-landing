Cushion mountains and a cave

18-21 monthsgross motor skillsno mess20 min

Build mountains of cushions and blankets on the rug: your baby climbs over them, crawls into a cave under you, and hides behind a mountain.

Steps

  1. Climb over the first mountain yourself: “Up and over.” Let your baby follow after you.

What you need

  • sofa cushions
  • blankets

Safety

Don’t use chairs, draped blankets or boxes on their side: keep everything on the floor.

Hold the cave position steadily and never lower yourself onto your baby, and stay away from furniture corners.

Building with sticks and clay

3-5 yearsthinking28 min

Connect cotton swabs with balls of modelling clay, compare a triangle to a square, and test your structure with a little book.

Setup

Roll 15 cherry-sized balls of modelling clay and set them on a piece of cardboard next to 20 cotton swabs.

Steps

  1. Stick 3 swabs into 3 balls: a triangle. Press on a corner: it holds its shape.
  2. Make a square from 4 swabs and balls and press on a corner: it folds sideways.
  3. Let your child add a diagonal swab to the square and test it again.
  4. Build side by side, each your own structure: your child chooses a tower, a house or a bridge.
  5. Set a small book on top. If the structure collapses, let your child decide how to reinforce it.

What you need

  • modelling clay
  • 20 cotton swabs
  • a piece of cardboard
  • a small book

Safety

Use cotton swabs instead of toothpicks, with no sharp ends.

Don’t let the clay go in the mouth, and pick up all the balls after playing if younger children are around.

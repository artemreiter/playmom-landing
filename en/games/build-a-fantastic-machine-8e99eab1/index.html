Build a fantastic machine

4-7 yearsindependence32 min

Your child builds a machine with an unusual job out of boxes and tubes, then switches it on and explains what it does.

Setup

Put boxes, kitchen roll tubes, foil and felt-tip pens on the table. Stick ten pieces of masking tape along the table edge.

Steps

  1. Suggest inventing a machine with an unusual job, like a hugging machine. Let your child think up their own.
  2. Your child picks the parts, tapes them together, draws on buttons and names the machine.
  3. Press a button and ask: “What happens now?” Your child voices the machine and says what it does.
  4. Swap: your child presses the button and you play the machine. Then they decide: finish it or build a new one.

What you need

  • empty cardboard boxes
  • kitchen roll tubes
  • foil
  • masking tape
  • felt-tip pens

Safety

Boxes with no staples or sharp edges; tear the tape by hand.

No string, elastic bands or small lids; if younger children are nearby, clear away the scraps.

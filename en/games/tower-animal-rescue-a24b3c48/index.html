Tower animal rescue

18-21 monthsmovementno mess14 min

Hide soft toys under low cushion towers, and your baby chooses who to rescue and takes the tower apart.

Steps

  1. Say: “The animals are stuck in the towers, who shall we rescue first?” and let your baby choose.

What you need

  • 4 cushions
  • 2 soft toys

Safety

Towers only from cushions lying flat, no higher than your baby’s knee, with no blankets on top.

Don’t put cushions on your baby or cover their face.

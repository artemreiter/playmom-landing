Flower shop

3-5 yearsthinkingno mess37 min

Your child makes paper flowers, arranges them in cups, and sells them to toy customers for cardboard coins.

Setup

Cut circles from coloured paper in 3 colours, green paper strips for stems, and cardboard coins. Set out plastic cups and sit 2 toys nearby.

Steps

  1. Tape a circle to a stem: the flower is ready. Let your child make 5 or 6 more.
  2. Let your child arrange the flowers in the cups, each colour in its own cup.
  3. Speak for the teddy bear: “I’d like one red flower.” Your child finds it, hands it over and gets a coin.
  4. Order a bouquet: “2 yellow and 1 blue.” Then swap roles: your child becomes the customer, and you’re the seller.

What you need

  • coloured paper
  • green paper
  • cardboard
  • tape
  • plastic cups
  • 2 toys
  • scissors

Safety

No glue or perfume needed; tape is enough.

Keep the scissors to yourself, and put the coins away after playing if younger children are around.

Who hid under the fabric

12-15 monthsspeechno mess12 min

Hide familiar toys under pieces of fabric with different textures, and let your child find them, then hide one themselves.

Steps

  1. In front of your child, cover a toy with fabric: “Oh, where did it go?”

What you need

  • 3 pieces of fabric with different textures
  • 2 toys bigger than your child's fist

Safety

Use only toys bigger than your child's fist, with no small parts.

Cover only the toys with the fabric, never your child's face or head.

Voices in the bottles

3-4 yearsspeechno mess23 min

Take turns shaking sealed bottles, listen to how each one sounds, and make up what invisible creature is hiding inside.

Setup

Put a handful of rice in one opaque bottle, dry pasta in a second and cotton balls in a third. Screw on the lids and wrap them with tape.

Steps

  1. Tell your child an invisible creature lives inside each bottle, and shake the first one.
  2. Ask how it sounds: does it jingle, rustle or rattle. Offer the word if your child is reaching for it.
  3. Let your child decide who it is and what it likes to do, while you draw the creature from their description.
  4. Swap roles: your child picks and shakes a bottle, and you make up the creature.

What you need

  • 3 opaque plastic bottles with lids
  • rice
  • dry pasta
  • cotton balls
  • tape
  • paper and a pencil

Safety

Don’t use buttons or coins, and don’t let your child taste the rice or pasta since they’re raw.

If the tape peels off or a bottle cracks, take it away, and keep the bottles out of reach after playing.

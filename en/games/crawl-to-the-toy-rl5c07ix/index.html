Crawl to the toy

6-9 monthsgross motor skillsno mess6 min

Put a bright toy just beyond your baby’s reach and call them to crawl or stretch their way to it.

Steps

  1. Lay your baby on their tummy, with the toy about 30 cm in front of their hands.

What you need

  • a soft play mat
  • 2 bright toys

Safety

The toys are bigger than your baby’s fist and have no small parts.

Stop if your baby gets tired or cries while on their tummy.

Sock puppet theatre

15-18 monthsfine motor skillsno mess20 min

The sock on your hand peeps out from behind a box and says “yum yum”, then your baby’s own sock comes to life.

Setup

Put a clean sock on your hand: fingers on top, thumb underneath, and there’s a mouth. Stand a box in front of you: that’s the stage.

Steps

  1. Hide your hand behind the box. Slowly raise the sock over the edge: “Peekaboo. I’m Mr Sock.”
  2. Let the sock open and close its mouth: “Yum, yum.” Tickle your baby’s palm with it.
  3. Hide the sock behind the box. Pop it up again when your baby looks or calls.
  4. Put the second sock on your baby’s hand. Let their sock peep out from behind the box too, and act surprised.

What you need

  • 2 clean socks
  • a box

Safety

A box with no staples or sharp edges; clean socks with no stickers or pompoms, worn only on hands, never on faces.

If your baby puts the sock in their mouth, take it off and carry on with yours.

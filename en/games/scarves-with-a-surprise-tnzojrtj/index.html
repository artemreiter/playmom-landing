Scarves with a surprise

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno mess12 min

Pull scarves out of a box together, and under the last one your baby finds a hidden toy.

Steps

  1. Slowly pull out the first scarf and wave it: “Peekaboo.”

Open in the app

What you need

  • a shoebox
  • three light scarves
  • a large soft toy

Safety

Use scarves with no fringes or ties; no ribbons. Don’t leave a scarf on your baby’s face.

The toy is bigger than your baby’s fist and has no small parts.

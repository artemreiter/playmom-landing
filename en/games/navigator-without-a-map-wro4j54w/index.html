Navigator without a map

5-6 yearsthinkingno mess15 min

Guide your child with closed eyes to a hidden toy using step by step commands, then they command you.

Setup

Clear anything to trip over from the path, and hide a small toy while your child isn't looking.

Steps

  1. Ask your child to close their eyes and give commands: “Two steps forward. Stop. Turn right.”
  2. Speak slowly and wait for each command to be done. At the treasure, say: “Crouch down and reach out your hand.”
  3. Swap: your child hides the toy and gives commands while you walk with closed eyes.
  4. For a new round, draw the route with arrows: let your child read them and lead you.

What you need

  • a small toy
  • a sheet of paper
  • a pencil

Safety

Don't use a blindfold: your child simply closes their eyes and can open them at any moment.

Walk alongside and stop your child near furniture and corners.

Guess by smell

24-30 monthssensesno mess25 min

Smell an orange, a banana and mint together, then let your toddler close their eyes and guess which one you hold under their nose.

Steps

  1. Smell each food yourself, name it and hold it under your toddler’s nose: “The orange smells like this.”

What you need

  • a plate

Safety

Keep the plate with you, and don’t eat the food during the game; skip ground spices, coffee and garlic.

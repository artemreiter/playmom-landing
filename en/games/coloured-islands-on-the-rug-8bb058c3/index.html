Coloured islands on the rug

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno mess11 min

Lay out coloured towels as islands on the rug, and let your toddler step or crawl from island to island, choosing where to go next.

Setup

Lay three or four different-coloured towels on the rug, one small step apart from each other.

Steps

  1. Stand on the first towel: “I’m on the red island,” and call your toddler over.
  2. Holding your toddler’s hand, step onto the next island, or let them crawl over if that’s easier.
  3. Ask: “Where now, the blue one or the yellow one?” and go wherever your toddler points.
  4. Rearrange the islands together with your toddler and walk the path again.

What you need

  • 3 or 4 different-coloured towels
  • a rug

Safety

Put the towels on the rug only, never a smooth floor; no jumping, and hold your toddler’s hand at every crossing.

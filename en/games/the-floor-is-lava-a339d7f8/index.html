The floor is lava

2-5 yearsmovementno mess15 min

The floor turns into lava, and your child crosses it on cushion islands, choosing their own route.

Setup

Lay 5 sofa cushions or folded towels on the rug, a step apart.

Steps

  1. Announce: “The floor is lava, you can only step on the islands.”
  2. Show the way yourself: step from island to island.
  3. Let your child choose the route and how to move: walking, sideways or on all fours. Older children can jump from standing.
  4. Let your child move one island and invent a new route for you.

What you need

  • 5 sofa cushions or folded towels
  • a rug

Safety

The islands lie on the rug a step apart; don’t play on a slippery floor.

Nobody climbs on the sofa, chairs or tables. If an island slides, put it back.

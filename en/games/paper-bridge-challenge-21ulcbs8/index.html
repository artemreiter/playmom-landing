Paper bridge challenge

5-6 yearsthinkingno mess28 min

Build a paper bridge between two stacks of books, then test with coins to see which design holds the most weight.

Steps

  1. Suggest your child connect the stacks with a paper bridge, built however they like.

What you need

  • five sheets of paper
  • tape
  • two equal stacks of books
  • 20 coins
  • a pencil and a sheet for keeping score

Safety

Coins don’t go in the mouth; count them after the game and keep them away from children under three.

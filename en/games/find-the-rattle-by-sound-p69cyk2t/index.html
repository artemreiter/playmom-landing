Find the rattle by sound

9-12 monthsthinkingno mess8 min

Hide a rattle under a scarf while your baby watches, shake it and let them find it by sound.

Setup

Sit on the floor facing your baby. Put two scarves and the rattle between you.

Steps

  1. Shake the rattle, then let your baby hold it and listen.
  2. While your baby watches, cover the rattle with a scarf and shake it underneath: “Where’s that sound?”
  3. Wait for your baby to pull the scarf off. Cheer at the find and let them shake the rattle.
  4. Hide it under the other scarf. Then let your baby cover the rattle while you search.

What you need

  • two light scarves or tea towels
  • a large rattle or squeaky toy

Safety

Use a large, sturdy rattle with no small parts, too big to fit in the mouth.

Never leave a scarf on your baby’s face, and put the scarves away after the game.

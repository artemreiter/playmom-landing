Rain of coloured drops

24-36 monthsfine motor skills17 min

Drip clean water onto felt-tip dots on kitchen roll and watch together as a rainbow spreads.

Steps

  1. Fill the pipette with water and drip it onto a dot: “Drip.” Show how the colour spreads.

What you need

  • kitchen roll
  • washable felt-tip pens
  • a plastic pipette
  • a small stable bowl
  • water
  • a wipe-clean tablecloth

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach, with just a little water in the bottom of the bowl.

The felt-tip pens stay with you; your child drips only clean water.

Brave traveller’s tunnel

30-36 monthsgross motor skillsno mess20 min

Drape a blanket over the dining table to make a tunnel, and invite your child to crawl through to the treasure in different ways.

Setup

Drape a blanket over the dining table so the edges hang down on the sides, leaving both ends open.

Steps

  1. Say that the tunnel only lets brave travellers through, and put a toy treasure at the far exit.
  2. Your child crawls toward the treasure while you wait to meet them at the exit.
  3. Let your child choose how to crawl next time: like a snake on their belly or a bear on all fours.
  4. Swap roles: your child hides the treasure, and you search by peeking in from both ends.

What you need

  • a dining table
  • a blanket
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Use the tunnel only under a sturdy table with no chairs or items on top, and keep both ends open.

Use a treasure bigger than your child’s fist, with no food or small parts.

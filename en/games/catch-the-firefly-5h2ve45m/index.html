Catch the firefly

18-21 monthsattentionno mess12 min

Move the flashlight spot over the wall and floor while your child walks or crawls after the firefly and covers it with a palm.

Steps

  1. Shine the light on the wall: “Look, a firefly.” Lower the spot to the floor in front of your child.

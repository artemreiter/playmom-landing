Potion with a secret rule

3-4 yearsthinkingno mess16 min

Make a magic potion from things around the house by a secret rule, then guess the rule your child came up with.

Setup

Put a sock, a cotton pad, a spoon, a jar lid, a wooden block and a piece of foil on the table, with a bowl beside them.

Steps

  1. Say: “The potion needs anything soft,” and drop the sock into the bowl.
  2. Let your child squeeze the other things and add everything soft to the potion.
  3. Name a new rule, such as “anything that rings,” and let your child test each thing by tapping or rustling it.
  4. Swap: your child secretly picks a rule and makes the potion, while you guess what the things have in common.

What you need

  • a sock
  • a cotton pad
  • a spoon
  • a jar lid
  • a wooden block
  • a piece of foil
  • a bowl

Safety

Leave out coins, beads, batteries and magnets, and give your child the foil as one large piece.

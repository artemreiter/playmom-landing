Dancing shadows

6-9 monthsattentionno messbedtime7 min

Dim the lights and show your baby the shadow of your hand on the ceiling: it flies, flies away and comes back.

Steps

  1. Hold the torch to one side and shine it at the ceiling. Wait for your baby to find the spot of light.

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a blanket

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover, to the side, not above your baby; never shine it in their eyes.

If the shadows upset your baby, turn the light on and stop playing.

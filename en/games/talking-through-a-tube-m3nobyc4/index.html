Talking through a tube

6-9 monthsspeechno mess8 min

Speak long, drawn-out sounds into a cardboard tube, wait for your baby to answer, and echo their sounds back like a real conversation.

Steps

  1. Hold the tube to your lips and say a long “Boo”: your voice sounds deep, like an echo in a cave.

Safety

Speak into the tube quietly, and never hold it up to your baby’s ear.

Shining stone in the cave

1-3 yearsmovementno mess19 min

Hide a sparkly toy in a blanket cave under the table and let your child crawl in for the shining stone.

Setup

Drape a duvet over the dining table, leaving the entrance open. Put a pillow inside and a sparkly toy in the far corner.

Steps

  1. Say: “A shining stone is hidden in the cave. Can you find it?” Lift the edge at the entrance.
  2. Let your child crawl inside, climb over the pillow and bring the stone out.
  3. Now your child hides the stone and you crawl in to look, following their hints.
  4. Ask who crawls in next and where to hide the stone.

What you need

  • a dining table
  • a duvet or throw
  • a pillow
  • a sparkly toy

Safety

Build the cave from a sturdy table and a duvet, with no chairs, and keep the entrance open.

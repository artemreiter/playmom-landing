Mood drums

2-4 yearsemotionsno mess17 min

Turn pots and bowls upside down, drum on them with spoons, and use the sounds together to show joy, sadness and anger.

Steps

  1. Drum on each one together and listen: which one rings, which one booms.

What you need

  • a pot
  • a plastic container
  • a metal bowl
  • a cardboard box
  • 2 wooden spoons

Safety

Use only unbreakable dishes, and don’t put film or rice on the drums.

If it’s too loud for your child, drum more quietly.

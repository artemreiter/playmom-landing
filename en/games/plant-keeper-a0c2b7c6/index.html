Plant keeper

4-7 yearsemotions20 min

Your child takes charge of a potted basil plant: watering it, drawing it once a week, and comparing how it grows.

Steps

  1. Tell your child they’re now the keeper of the plant, and let them give it a name.

What you need

  • a potted basil plant
  • a small watering can
  • a notebook
  • a ruler
  • coloured pencils

Safety

The plant is non-toxic; leaves and soil don’t go in the mouth, and you both wash your hands after watering.

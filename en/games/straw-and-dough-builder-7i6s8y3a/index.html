Straw and dough builder

2-4 yearsthinking30 min

Stick whole straws into balls of play dough and build a triangle together on the table, then a little house.

Setup

Roll 5 balls of play dough about the size of a small orange, and set 6 whole plastic straws next to them.

Steps

  1. Poke a straw into a ball: “Poke.” Let your child poke a second straw into another ball.
  2. Build a triangle together on the table from 3 straws and 3 balls: “This is the roof.”
  3. Let your child add 2 balls and 3 straws underneath: now it’s a little house.
  4. Take the house apart, and let your child choose what to build next: a fence, a road or a new house.

What you need

  • play dough
  • 6 plastic straws

Safety

Use play dough labelled for your child’s age, keep the balls bigger than their fist, and don’t let them put the dough in their mouth.

Use only whole straws, no scissors involved. Play seated, and replace any straw right away if it gets chewed.

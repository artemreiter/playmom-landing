Treasure dig

9-12 monthsfine motor skillsno mess13 min

Hide large toys under scrunched-up paper in a box, and your baby digs through the paper balls and finds the treasure.

Setup

Put two toys bigger than your baby’s fist in the bottom of a box with no staples, and cover them with 5-6 whole sheets of paper scrunched into balls.

Steps

  1. Take out one ball while your baby watches: “Who’s hiding in there?”
  2. Help your baby take out balls until a toy appears, and name it.
  3. Let your baby touch the toy, then hide it under the paper again.
  4. Let your baby choose which toy to hide, or hide it themselves, and you search.

What you need

  • a cardboard box
  • 5-6 sheets of paper
  • 2 large toys

Safety

If your baby tears off bits of paper or puts it in their mouth, take them away and pick up the scraps after every round.

Large toys only, with no small parts.

Swim under the wave

9-12 monthsgross motor skillsno mess16 min

Hold a scarf low over the floor like a wave, and your baby crawls under it into your arms.

Setup

Spread a duvet on the floor and sit your baby at one end. Kneel at the other end and hold a light scarf by both ends.

Steps

  1. Stretch the scarf out in front of you about 30 cm high and call your baby: “Swim to me under the wave.”
  2. If your baby lifts up, raise the scarf higher so the fabric doesn’t touch their head.
  3. When your baby reaches you, give them a hug and say: “Made it.”
  4. Show two scarves in different colours: whichever one your baby touches is the one they swim under next.

What you need

  • 2 light scarves
  • a duvet

Safety

Always hold the scarf yourself: don’t tie it to chairs or lay it over your baby’s face or head.

No bells, cords or clothes pegs.

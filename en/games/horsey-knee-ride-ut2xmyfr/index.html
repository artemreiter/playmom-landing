Horsey knee ride

9-12 monthsmovementno prepno mess4 min

Sit your baby on your knees facing you and rock them gently up and down to a little riding song, “Off we go, off we go.”

Setup

No preparation needed.

Steps

  1. Sit on a sofa or chair with your feet flat on the floor, and sit your baby on your knees facing you.
  2. Hold your baby firmly under the arms, and gently raise and lower your heels.
  3. Sing “Off we go, off we go, trotting to the forest for nuts” and rock in time, sometimes slower, sometimes a little faster.
  4. Stop and wait: if your baby bounces, reaches out or looks at you, the little horse sets off again.

Safety

Hold your baby by the body, not by the hands, and rock smoothly, with no bouncing or jerking.

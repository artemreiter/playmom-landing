Caterpillar on a spoon

24-30 monthsfine motor skillsno mess10 min

Thread toilet paper tubes onto the handle of a wooden spoon together and walk the finished caterpillar across the table.

Setup

Draw eyes and a smile with a marker on the wide end of a wooden spoon, and put 3 toilet paper tubes beside it.

Steps

  1. Show the spoon: “This is the caterpillar’s head,” and thread one tube onto the handle.
  2. Hold the spoon by the head while your baby threads on the next tubes.
  3. Once all the tubes are on, walk the caterpillar across the table and up your baby’s arm: “Tap, tap, tap.”
  4. Take the tubes off and swap: your baby holds the head, and you thread the tubes on in the order they show you.

What you need

  • a wooden spoon
  • 3 toilet paper tubes
  • a marker

Safety

Instead of pasta or pipe cleaners, use only whole tubes, they’re bigger than your baby’s fist.

Put the marker away before playing, and if your baby chews the cardboard, take away the soggy tube.

Colours running on milk

12-15 monthsfine motor skills13 min

Drop food colouring onto milk and touch it with a soapy cotton bud wherever your baby points, so the colours rush away.

Setup

Pour a thin layer of whole milk onto a dinner plate and put it on the table on your side. Add three drops of food colouring in different colours and different places.

Steps

  1. Show the coloured drops and ask where to touch. Let your baby point with a finger.
  2. Dip the tip of a cotton bud in washing-up liquid and touch the milk at that spot: “They ran away.”
  3. Keep going while your baby picks new spots and colours.
  4. Lay a sheet of paper on the milk, lift it off yourself and show your baby the print.

What you need

  • whole milk
  • a dinner plate
  • food colouring in three colours
  • washing-up liquid
  • a cotton bud
  • a sheet of paper
  • a baby wipe

Safety

Keep the plate, milk and washing-up liquid on your side, out of your baby’s reach; only you touch them.

After the print, wipe your baby’s hands straight away and pour the milk away.

Blanket trampoline

4-5 yearsgross motor skillsno mess18 min

Stretch a blanket together with your child and toss soft heroes up, trying to catch them back on the trampoline.

Setup

Clear a space away from furniture, lamps and windows. Take a blanket and 3 soft toys.

Steps

  1. Hold opposite edges of the blanket with your child and pull it taut. Place the first hero in the middle.
  2. On the count of “three”, jerk the blanket up together: the hero flies up. Try to catch it back on the blanket.
  3. Let your child pick the next hero and say how high to toss it: a little or high.
  4. Count how many times in a row the hero lands on the trampoline, and try to beat your record.

What you need

  • a blanket or sheet
  • 3 soft toys

Safety

Toss only soft toys, never hard or heavy objects.

Hold the blanket and stay in place, no walking backward.

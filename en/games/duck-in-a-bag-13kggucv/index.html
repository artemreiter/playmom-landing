Duck in a bag

6-9 monthssenses17 min

Tape a bag of water and a toy to the table, then push it around together with your baby by pressing on the bag with your palms.

Steps

  1. Press on the bag with your palm: the water shifts, and the duck floats away.

What you need

  • a large sturdy zip-top bag
  • water
  • a rubber duck
  • wide tape

Safety

Keep only water and the duck inside: no buttons, glitter, gel or oil.

Stay within arm’s reach, and take the bag away once you’re done playing.

If the bag tears, remove it right away.

Calm-down bottle

21-24 monthsemotionsno mess13 min

Shake a sealed bottle of water and oil together and watch the coloured drops slowly sink.

Setup

Fill a plastic bottle two-thirds full of water, add a few drops of food colouring and top up with vegetable oil almost to the brim. Screw the lid on and wrap it with tape.

Steps

  1. Show the bottle and slowly turn it upside down: the coloured drops will float through the oil.
  2. Give the bottle to your baby: let them shake it or roll it on the floor.
  3. Watch the drops sink together. Say quietly: “The drops have calmed down, and so have we.”
  4. Ask who shakes it next, you or your baby, and watch again.

What you need

  • a clear plastic bottle
  • water
  • vegetable oil
  • food colouring
  • sticky tape

Safety

Before each game, check that the lid is taped shut and the bottle doesn’t leak.

Your baby plays with the bottle only while you’re there; afterwards, put it somewhere high.

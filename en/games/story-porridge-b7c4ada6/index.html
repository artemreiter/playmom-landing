Story porridge

3-4 yearsspeechno mess20 min

Take turns dropping toys and objects into a saucepan, and each one adds a new event to your shared story.

Setup

Put a saucepan and a spoon on the floor, with 6 different toys and objects beside them, each bigger than your child’s fist.

Steps

  1. Put the first object in the saucepan and start the story: “Once upon a time there was a sock that dreamed of flying.”
  2. Let your child pick the next object, drop it into the porridge and say what happened next.
  3. Stir with the spoon and add objects in turn: each one brings one new event.
  4. When the objects run out, pretend to taste the porridge while your child tells how the story ended.

What you need

  • a saucepan
  • a spoon
  • 6 toys and objects bigger than your child’s fist

Safety

Leave out buttons, pebbles, cotton wool balls and pasta: only big toys and objects.

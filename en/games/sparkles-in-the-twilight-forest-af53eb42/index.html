Sparkles in the twilight forest

3-4 yearsattentionno mess22 min

Your child directs the torch beam and collects sparkly foil balls hidden around a dim room.

Setup

Roll six tangerine-sized balls of foil, hide them near the floor, and close the curtains while keeping the floor visible.

Steps

  1. Say that sparkles are lost in the twilight forest, and switch on the magic torch.
  2. Let your child give directions, “Shine under the chair,” while you aim the beam there.
  3. When a sparkle glints, your child walks over to it and puts it in the box.
  4. Swap roles: your child hides the sparkles and gives hints of “warmer” or “colder” while you search with the torch.

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • foil
  • a box

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover, and never shine it in anyone’s eyes.

Hide the sparkles low down so no one has to climb on furniture, and walk, don’t run.

Cakes for the elf

24-30 monthsfine motor skillsno mess12 min

Sort sock balls into the cups of a muffin tin like little cakes together and treat a toy elf to one.

Steps

  1. Say: “The elf ordered a cake,” put a sock ball in a cup and announce: “Ready.”

What you need

  • 6 pairs of socks
  • a muffin tin
  • a bowl
  • a soft toy

Safety

Don’t use pompoms or small balls, only sock balls bigger than your baby’s fist.

Choose a tin with no sharp edges, or use 6 plastic bowls instead.

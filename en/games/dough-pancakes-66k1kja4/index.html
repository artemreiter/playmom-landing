Dough pancakes

15-18 monthssenses22 min

Roll balls of homemade salt-free dough and flatten them into pancakes with your palm, stacking them up.

Steps

  1. Roll a ball the size of a satsuma and flatten it with your palm: “Pancake.”

What you need

  • flour
  • water
  • vegetable oil
  • a wipeable cloth

Safety

The dough has no salt, dye or fragrance; if your baby pulls raw dough towards their mouth, take the piece away.

Don’t put pasta or small moulds into the dough.

If your baby is allergic to wheat, use rice flour instead.

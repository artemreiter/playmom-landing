Cardboard box tunnel

18-21 monthsgross motor skillsno mess30 min

Join 2 big boxes into a straight tunnel on the floor, and call your little one to crawl through to you at the other end.

Setup

Cut the bottom and top off 2 big boxes with no staples, tape over the edges, and join them into a straight tunnel on the floor.

Steps

  1. Sit at the far end, peek into the tunnel and call: “Peekaboo, crawl to me.”
  2. When your little one crawls out, give them a hug and show them the toy waiting at the exit.
  3. Roll a big ball into the tunnel and let your little one crawl after it.
  4. Let your little one choose which end to crawl from, while you wait at the other.

What you need

  • 2 big cardboard boxes
  • wide tape
  • a utility knife for the adult
  • a big ball
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Keep the boxes flat on the floor: don’t put anything on top or cover them with a blanket.

Use a ball and toy bigger than your little one’s fist, and put the knife away before playing.

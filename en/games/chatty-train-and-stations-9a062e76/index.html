Chatty train and stations

21-24 monthsspeechno mess17 min

Your child pushes a box carriage around the room, and at each station you name objects and your child picks a passenger.

Setup

Set up 3 stations in different corners of the room, each with 2 large things: a cup, a soft toy, a hat. The carriage is a large box.

Steps

  1. Sit a soft toy in the box: “Choo-choo, the train is off.” Your child pushes the carriage.
  2. “Stop, station.” Name an object: “Cup. Blue cup.” Wait to see if your child repeats it or points.
  3. Let your child choose one object and put it in the carriage as a passenger.
  4. “Toot-toot,” and off you go again. At the last station your child says “stop” or waves.

What you need

  • a large cardboard box
  • a soft toy
  • 6 large things from around the house

Safety

Only large things at the stations, no small figures or toy cars.

The box has no staples or sharp edges: your child pushes it and doesn’t climb inside.

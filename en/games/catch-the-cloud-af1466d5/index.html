Catch the cloud

30-36 monthsmovementno mess12 min

Toss a light scarf like a cloud and take turns catching it as it floats slowly down.

Steps

  1. Toss the scarf above your child: “The cloud is flying.” Let your child catch the cloud before it lands.

What you need

Safety

Don’t knot the scarf or wrap it around anyone’s neck.

Catch on a flat floor, without running towards furniture or jumping off anything raised.

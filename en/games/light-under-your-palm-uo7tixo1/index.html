Light under your palm

9-12 monthsthinkingno messbedtime10 min

Hold a torch pointing at the ceiling, and let your baby cover it with a palm, a scarf or a cup to watch the light go out.

Setup

Dim the lights so the floor is still visible. Get a soft, low-glare torch with a screwed-on battery cover.

Steps

  1. Sit next to your baby and point the torch at the ceiling: there’s the bright spot.
  2. Cover the torch with your palm: it goes dark. Take your hand away: the light comes back.
  3. Rest your baby’s palm on the torch, and it glows red. Let them move their hand on and off themselves.
  4. Hand over a scarf and a cup: let your baby choose what to cover the light with, while the torch stays in your hand.

What you need

  • a soft, low-glare LED torch with a screwed-on cover
  • a light scarf
  • a plastic cup

Safety

The torch stays in your hand the whole time, pointed at the ceiling and never at anyone’s eyes.

Never put the scarf over your baby’s face or head, and turn the lights back on if they feel uneasy in the dim room.

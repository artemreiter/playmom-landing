Rustling path

24-30 monthssensesno mess32 min

Crawl together along a path of paper, cardboard and a towel, listen to how each sheet sounds, and take turns guessing which one it is.

Steps

  1. Crawl along the path first and touch each sheet with your palm: “It rustles,” “It crinkles,” “It’s quiet.”

What you need

  • a newspaper
  • a sheet of baking paper
  • a sheet of cardboard
  • a towel
  • masking tape

Safety

Leave out bags and any film, including bubble wrap: they can be pulled over the face or bitten into.

Pick up any torn-off scraps of paper right away.

Sad bunny, happy teddy

24-36 monthsemotionsno mess14 min

Act out short scenes with two soft toy animals in which your child comforts the bunny and shares the teddy’s joy.

Setup

Sit two soft toy animals in front of your child, for example a bunny and a teddy.

Steps

  1. The bunny walks along and falls on its side: “Bump. Bunny is hurt and sad.” Ask your child: “What shall we do?”
  2. Let your child comfort the bunny however they like: stroke it, pick it up, hug it. The bunny smiles: “Thank you, I feel better.”
  3. Teddy finds a pretend berry and shares half with the bunny: “Here, have some.” They’re both happy.
  4. Give your child one animal and take the other. Let them show whether their animal is sad or happy, and yours responds.

What you need

  • two soft toy animals

Safety

Soft toys with no small parts that could come off; the berry is only pretend.

If the sad scene upsets your child, move straight on to a happy one.

Pop the rainbow clouds

12-15 monthsgross motor skills10 min

Blow bubbles above your baby and help them pop the bubbles with a palm and a finger.

Steps

  1. Blow a few bubbles above your baby: “Here come the clouds.”

What you need

  • a tub of bubbles with a wand
  • a blanket

Safety

Keep the tub with you and don’t blow bubbles into your baby’s face. If bubble mixture gets in their eyes, rinse them with water.

Play on the blanket or outside: the floor under bubbles gets slippery.

Invisible lemon ink

5-6 yearsthinking25 min

Your child writes a secret message in lemon juice, then you reveal it with a warm iron, and the letters appear in brown.

Setup

Squeeze half a lemon’s juice into a saucer, with cotton swabs, paper and a towel nearby.

Steps

  1. Your child dips a swab in the juice and writes a secret message on paper: a name, a word or a drawing.
  2. Once the paper dries, lay it on the towel and press a warm iron over it.
  3. Your child watches from across the table as the message appears, and says what it is.
  4. Swap roles: you write a secret message, your child guesses what it says, then reveal the paper together.

What you need

  • half a lemon
  • a saucer
  • cotton swabs
  • white paper
  • a towel
  • an iron

Safety

Only you handle the iron; unplug it after and let it cool out of your child’s reach.

Use the swab only on paper, never near eyes, ears or mouth.

If your child is allergic to citrus, use milk instead.

Will it stand or fall

12-15 monthsthinkingno mess11 min

Stack a block on a block, on an upside-down cup and on a towel, and watch together where the tower stands and where it falls.

Steps

  1. Stand a block on a block: “It stands.” Give your baby a third block to put on top.

What you need

  • 3-4 large blocks

Safety

Blocks bigger than your baby’s fist; no metal lids or sponges.

Build on the floor, with the tower no higher than your baby’s chest when sitting.

Catching light on fabrics

12-15 monthssensesno mess28 min

Shine a torch on different fabrics on the floor, and your baby catches the beam with a hand and feels smooth and fluffy.

Setup

Draw the curtains, leaving the room dim, and lay a towel, a smooth scarf and a knitted scarf side by side on the floor.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch on the towel and say: “The light has landed on something soft.”
  2. When your baby pats the light, name the fabric under their hand: soft, smooth or scratchy.
  3. Move the light slowly from fabric to fabric so your baby can catch up with it.
  4. Let your baby put a hand on any fabric, and send the light there.

What you need

  • a torch
  • a towel
  • a smooth scarf
  • a knitted scarf

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down cover; don’t shine it in your baby’s eyes.

No threads, ribbons, cords or beads; don’t drape the fabrics over your baby’s face or head.

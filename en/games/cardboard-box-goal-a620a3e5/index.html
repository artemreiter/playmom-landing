Cardboard box goal

24-30 monthsgross motor skillsno mess12 min

Lay a box on its side as a goal and take turns scoring with a soft ball by foot or hand, then let your little one play goalkeeper.

Steps

  1. Stand 2 steps from the goal, push the ball in with your foot, and say: “Goal.”

What you need

  • a big cardboard box
  • a soft ball

Safety

Use a soft ball bigger than your little one’s fist, and a box with no staples or sharp edges.

Clear small toys off the floor so your little one doesn’t trip while kicking.

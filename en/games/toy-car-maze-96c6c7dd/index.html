Toy car maze

30-36 monthsindependenceno mess20 min

Stick a simple maze of masking tape on the floor, and let your baby guide a toy from the entrance to the exit on their own.

Setup

Stick a simple maze of masking tape on the floor, with no dead ends and a clear entrance and exit.

Steps

  1. Let your baby choose who got lost: a big toy car or a figure.
  2. Show how to guide the toy between the lines toward the exit.
  3. From here, let your baby guide it themselves, and only prompt them with a question: “Which way now?”
  4. Celebrate at the exit, then add one more fork in the path for a new round.
  5. Swap roles: your baby shows the way, while your toy deliberately turns the wrong way.

What you need

  • masking tape
  • a big toy car or figure

Safety

The toys are bigger than your baby’s fist, and any torn-off bits of tape get picked up right away.

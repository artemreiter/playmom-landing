Mood book with a mirror

12-15 monthsemotionsno messbedtime27 min

Flip through a cardboard book of family faces together, copy their expressions, and end by finding your little one in a mirror.

Setup

Glue 4 family photos, each showing a different feeling, onto card, and tape the pages together into a book. Put an unbreakable kid’s mirror nearby.

Steps

  1. Turn the pages one at a time and name the feeling: “Grandma’s laughing, she’s happy.”
  2. Copy each face yourself: drop your lips for sad, round your mouth for surprised.
  3. At the end, hold up the mirror: “And who’s this? It’s you,” and smile into it together.
  4. Turn the pages again, letting your little one flip them and choose who to look at next.

What you need

  • 4 photos of family members
  • cardboard from a box
  • wide clear tape
  • a glue stick
  • scissors
  • an unbreakable kid’s mirror

Safety

Skip ribbons, rings and a glass mirror, and put glue and scissors away before playing.

If your little one chews a page or the tape peels off, take the book away.

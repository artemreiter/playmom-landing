Dance and freeze

12-15 monthsgross motor skillsno prepno mess8 min

Dance to an upbeat song, copy each other’s moves and freeze whenever the music stops.

Steps

  1. Stand in front of your baby, sway and clap along to the beat.

What you need

Safety

Don’t pull or swing your baby by the arms: dance alongside them or hold them under the arms.

Keep the music quiet, and skip spinning if your baby isn’t yet steady on their feet.

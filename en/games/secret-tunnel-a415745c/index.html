Secret tunnel

6-9 monthsthinkingno mess7 min

Hide a scarf in a cardboard tube, and your baby tugs the corner and pulls it out.

Steps

  1. Hold the tube upright in front of your baby and wiggle the corner: “Who’s hiding in there?”

What you need

  • a kitchen roll tube
  • 2 thin scarves

Safety

Hide only a large scarf in the tube: your baby could swallow anything small enough to fit through it.

Don’t drape the scarf over your baby’s face or head.

Cushion and towel course

9-12 monthsgross motor skillsno mess18 min

Crawl with your baby over a roll, a rustling towel and a cushion hill to their favourite toy at the finish.

Setup

Lay out in a row on the floor: a rolled-up towel, the second towel with a paper bag under it, a sofa cushion and an upside-down washing-up bowl.

Steps

  1. Show your baby two toys: whichever one they take goes at the finish.
  2. Crawl alongside: over the roll, “climb over”, along the towel, “swish, swish”, onto the cushion, “up the hill”.
  3. Go round the bowl and tap it with your palm: “Boom.”
  4. At the finish your baby takes the toy. On the next lap let them crawl in front while you catch up.

What you need

  • 2 bath towels
  • a sturdy paper bag
  • a sofa cushion
  • a plastic washing-up bowl
  • 2 toys

Safety

No elastic bands, ribbons, bells or plastic bags: the bag is paper only and lies under the towel.

Everything lies flat on the floor; stay within arm’s reach.

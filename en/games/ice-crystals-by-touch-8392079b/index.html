Ice crystals by touch

3-4 yearsindependenceno messbedtime17 min

Your child helps a polar explorer: without looking, they search a bag for smooth, round and soft crystals and sort them.

Setup

Put 5 different things bigger than your child’s fist into a pillowcase or cloth bag: a block, a ball, a sock, a spoon, a pine cone.

Steps

  1. Explain: a polar explorer has collected ice crystals but can’t sort them. Your child is the helper.
  2. Your child puts a hand in the bag and, without looking, searches for the smoothest crystal. They pull it out and check.
  3. New tasks: “Find a round one,” “Find a soft one.” Your child sorts the finds into two piles: soft and hard.
  4. Swap: your child sets the task, and you feel for it and describe what you can feel.

What you need

  • a pillowcase or cloth bag
  • 5 things bigger than your child’s fist

Safety

Only large things with no sharp edges go in the bag: no buttons, beads or small pebbles.

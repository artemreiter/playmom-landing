Parcel from the bear

6-9 monthsemotionsno mess18 min

Unwrap a towel parcel together with your baby and greet the toy that appears from inside.

Setup

Wrap two or three soft toys in separate tea towels, loosely and with no knots.

Steps

  1. Bring the parcel over saying “Knock-knock, a delivery,” and set it down in front of your baby.
  2. Pull the corner of the towel together, and let your baby tug off the last bit themselves.
  3. When a little face appears, name the toy and act out its feeling with your own face: “The bear is smiling.”
  4. Let your baby hug the toy, then set out two parcels: whichever one they touch is the one you open.

What you need

  • two or three soft toys bigger than your baby’s fist
  • two or three tea towels

Safety

The toys are bigger than your baby’s fist, with no buttons or small parts.

Never cover your baby’s face or head with the towel.

Patterns made of straws

30-36 monthsfine motor skillsno mess17 min

Your toddler pokes straws into holes in a box lid and arranges them into a sun, a fence or falling rain.

Setup

Beforehand, poke about 12 holes in a shoebox lid with a pencil, and put 10 plastic drinking straws nearby.

Steps

  1. Poke a straw into a hole: “Ooh, a little tree grew.”
  2. Hand your toddler a straw and let them choose any hole themselves.
  3. Once several straws are in, ask what it looks like: the sun, a fence or falling rain.
  4. Add straws in turns, then let your toddler pull them out and start a new pattern.

What you need

  • a shoebox with a lid
  • 10 plastic drinking straws
  • a pencil for the adult

Safety

Put the pencil away right after preparing the box, and use only a box with no staples or sharp edges.

Play sitting down and don’t walk around with a straw in the mouth; replace any straw that gets bitten right away.

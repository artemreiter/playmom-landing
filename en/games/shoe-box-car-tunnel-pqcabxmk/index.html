Shoe box car tunnel

18-21 monthsthinkingno mess35 min

Send a toy car down a ramp into a shoe box tunnel, and wait together for it to roll out the other side.

Steps

  1. Let the car go from the top: it disappears into the tunnel. “Where’s the car? There it is.”

What you need

  • a shoe box
  • a chopping board
  • a sofa cushion
  • a big toy car
  • a ball
  • scissors

Safety

The car and ball should both be bigger than your baby’s fist with no small parts; skip small marbles.

Keep the scissors to yourself, make sure the box has no staples, and don’t let your baby climb onto the ramp.

Bottle bowling

21-24 monthsgross motor skillsno mess23 min

Take turns rolling a ball down a towel lane toward empty bottles, and count together how many pins fall.

Setup

Set up 6 empty plastic bottles with no caps on the floor in a triangle. Spread a towel in front of them as a lane about 1.5 metres long.

Steps

  1. Sit on the floor at the start of the lane and roll the ball toward the bottles with both hands.
  2. Count the fallen bottles out loud: “One, two.”
  3. Hand the ball to your baby: let them roll it themselves, while you count together.
  4. Let your baby set the bottles back up however they like, and take turns rolling.

What you need

  • 6 empty plastic bottles
  • a soft ball
  • a towel

Safety

Keep the bottles empty and capless: no water, food colouring or lids needed.

Use a soft ball that’s bigger than your baby’s fist.

Surprise boxes

6-9 monthsemotionsno mess10 min

Open two little boxes: greet one find with a smile and the other with surprise, and your baby chooses which to open again.

Setup

Put a ball in one small box with a lid and a rattle in another. Both toys are bigger than your baby’s fist.

Steps

  1. Show the first box: “What’s in here?” Open it slowly, take out the ball and smile broadly: “Hooray.”
  2. Open the second: eyebrows up, mouth round, “Oh.” Shake the rattle softly.
  3. Close both boxes and put them in front of your baby. Wait to see which one they reach for.
  4. Open the chosen box and repeat its feeling: a smile for the ball, surprise for the rattle.

What you need

  • 2 small boxes with lids
  • a ball
  • a rattle

Safety

Use boxes with no staples or sharp edges.

Keep your surprise gentle and quiet; if your baby startles, stick to the smile.

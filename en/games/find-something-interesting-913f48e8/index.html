Find something interesting

24-30 monthsindependenceno mess23 min

Your child chooses a spoon, tongs or their hands and digs hidden toys out of a bowl of scrunched-up paper.

Steps

  1. Show the bowl: “There’s treasure hidden in here.” Let your child choose what to search with: the spoon, the tongs or their hands.

What you need

  • a washing-up bowl or box
  • 8 sheets of paper
  • 4 toys bigger than your child’s fist
  • a large spoon
  • kitchen tongs

Safety

No rice or other grains, sand, buttons or pebbles: only paper and large toys.

Plastic tongs with no sharp teeth. If your child puts paper in their mouth, take it away.

Smiles in the mirror

6-9 monthsemotionsno prepno mess6 min

Show your baby a smile and a surprised face in the mirror, wait for a response, then copy your baby.

Setup

Sit in front of a large mirror holding your baby, or set a shatterproof baby mirror in front of them.

Steps

  1. Wave at the reflection: “Hi, baby.” Wait for your baby to find you in the mirror.
  2. Give the mirror a big smile: “Smile.” Pause and see how your baby responds.
  3. Raise your eyebrows, make your mouth round and say: “Oh.” Wait again.
  4. Now your baby leads: if they smile or open their mouth, do the same and name it.

What you need

  • a large wall mirror or a shatterproof baby mirror

Safety

Use a shatterproof baby mirror or a large mirror fixed to the wall.

Stop if your baby turns away or seems tired.

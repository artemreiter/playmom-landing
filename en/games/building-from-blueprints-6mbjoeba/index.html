Building from blueprints

3-4 yearsthinkingno mess25 min

Draw simple blueprints of block structures, let your child build them, then draw a blueprint of their own creation.

Setup

Draw simple block structures on five cards: a three-block tower, a bridge, a staircase and two more of your choice.

Steps

  1. Put the first card next to the blocks and point to the bottom block in the drawing.
  2. Your child places the blocks to match the picture, while you point to which one comes next.
  3. Compare the build with the card: “Does it match?” Let your child choose the next card themselves.
  4. Swap roles: your child builds their own creation, you draw a blueprint of it, and you check it together.

What you need

  • 8 to 10 blocks in two or three colours
  • five cardboard cards
  • a marker

Safety

If children under three are playing nearby, use blocks bigger than their fist.

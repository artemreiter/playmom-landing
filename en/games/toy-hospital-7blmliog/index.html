Toy hospital

21-24 monthsemotionsno mess20 min

Your child treats teddy and the doll with a scarf compress and pretend medicine, then becomes your doctor.

Setup

Sit 2-3 soft toys in a row. Put a scarf and a teaspoon next to them.

Steps

  1. Say: “Oh, teddy hurt his paw, he’s sad.” Make a sad face.
  2. Let your child put the scarf on teddy’s paw and blow: “Whoo.” Teddy cheers up: “Thank you, doctor.”
  3. The doll sneezes: “Atishoo.” Let your child give her pretend medicine from the empty spoon.
  4. Ask who to treat next and let your child choose the patient. Then swap: you’re the patient, your child is the doctor.

What you need

  • 2-3 soft toys
  • a scarf
  • a teaspoon

Safety

Medicine is only pretend, from an empty spoon; keep real medicines and plasters out of reach.

Lay the scarf on the toy; don’t tie it or wrap it round.

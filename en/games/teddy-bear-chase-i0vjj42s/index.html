Teddy bear chase

21-24 monthsgross motor skillsno mess9 min

A teddy bear in your hands slowly chases your little one, then your little one takes the teddy and chases you.

Setup

Clear tripping hazards off the floor and get a plush teddy bear.

Steps

  1. Say in the teddy’s voice: “I’ll catch you, I’ll catch you,” and slowly walk the teddy toward your little one.
  2. Let your little one run away while the teddy follows slowly behind and stops now and then.
  3. When the teddy catches up, hug your little one together with the teddy and tickle them gently.
  4. Swap roles: give your little one the teddy, run away in small steps, and let them catch you.

What you need

  • a plush teddy bear

Safety

Keep the teddy moving slowly, so your little one can walk or jog without falling.

Tickle gently, and stop right away if your little one turns away or frowns.

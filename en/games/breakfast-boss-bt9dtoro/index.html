Breakfast boss

5-8 yearsindependence30 min

Your child chooses breakfast, makes it without the hob, lays the table, calls the family and tidies up afterwards.

Setup

The evening before, choose a breakfast together: porridge with fruit, sandwiches or yoghurt with cereal.

Steps

  1. Let your child draw a menu, then in the morning take out the food and set it out on their own.
  2. Your child makes it on their own: slicing a banana with a plastic knife, pouring cereal. Help only if asked.
  3. Your child lays the table: a plate, a spoon and a napkin for everyone.
  4. Let your child call the family and tell them what they made and why.
  5. After breakfast, your child takes the plates to the sink and wipes the table with a sponge.

What you need

  • food for the chosen breakfast
  • a plastic knife
  • plates and spoons
  • a sponge
  • paper and pencils

Safety

Only an adult handles the hob, the kettle, anything hot and sharp knives.

If anyone in the family has an allergy, check the menu together in advance.

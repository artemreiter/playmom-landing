Warm scarf rain

12-15 monthssensesno prepno messbedtime9 min

Pull a soft scarf out of a basket together and let it fall like a gentle rain onto your baby’s hands and feet.

Steps

  1. Show the end of the scarf and let your baby pull it: the whole scarf comes sliding out of the basket.

What you need

  • a soft scarf
  • a basket or basin

Safety

Lower the scarf only onto hands and feet, never onto the face or head, and never wrap it around the neck.

Your baby sits on the floor next to the basket, not inside it.

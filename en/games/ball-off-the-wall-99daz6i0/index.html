Ball off the wall

24-30 monthsmovementno mess11 min

Take turns rolling a ball down the hallway to the wall, catch it on the bounce, then aim it between two cushions.

Steps

  1. Stand together at the start of the hallway and push the ball toward the wall with both hands.

What you need

  • a light ball, 15 to 20 cm
  • two cushions
  • a clear hallway

Safety

The ball is light and bigger than your baby’s fist, and the hallway is clear of anything to trip over.

Don’t race to the wall or rush your baby.

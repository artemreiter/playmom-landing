Ball off the wall

24-30 monthsmovementno mess11 min

Take turns rolling a ball down the hallway to the wall, catch it on the bounce, then aim it between two cushions.

Setup

Clear shoes and clutter out of the hallway, and set a light ball and two cushions nearby.

Steps

  1. Stand together at the start of the hallway and push the ball toward the wall with both hands.
  2. When the ball bounces back, say “Catch it,” and let your baby walk or run to meet it.
  3. Now let your baby roll the ball, while you catch the bounce and roll it back.
  4. Set two cushions along the hallway: your baby decides where to roll from so the ball passes between them.

What you need

  • a light ball, 15 to 20 cm
  • two cushions
  • a clear hallway

Safety

The ball is light and bigger than your baby’s fist, and the hallway is clear of anything to trip over.

Don’t race to the wall or rush your baby.

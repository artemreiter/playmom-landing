Scarves into a bottle

12-15 monthsfine motor skillsno messbedtime13 min

Scrunch up thin scarves together and push them through the neck of a clear bottle, then watch them swirl around inside.

Setup

Get an empty 1.5 litre clear bottle with no lid, and 4 thin scarves in different colours.

Steps

  1. Scrunch a scarf into your fist and push it through the neck: “Poof, it’s gone in.”
  2. Give your baby a scarf: let them scrunch and push it in themselves. If it doesn’t go, gently guide their hand.
  3. Shake the bottle together and watch the scarves swirl around inside.
  4. Pull the scarves out with your fingers and lay them out in front of your baby: let them choose which one goes in first.

What you need

  • an empty 1.5 litre clear bottle
  • 4 thin scarves

Safety

Keep the lid and pompoms out of reach: use only scarves at least as big as an adult’s palm.

Hide behind the curtain

24-30 monthsemotionsno prepno mess9 min

Your child hides behind a curtain with their feet showing, you search all the wrong places and find them, then you hide.

Setup

No preparation needed. Choose a curtain at a closed window, with no blind cords nearby.

Steps

  1. Show your child how to stand behind the curtain so their feet peek out.
  2. Ask loudly: “Where’s my little one?” Walk around the room and look in the wrong places: under the table, behind the armchair.
  3. Go to the curtain, spot the feet and act surprised: “There you are.” Give a hug if your child wants one.
  4. Swap: now you hide behind the curtain with your feet in view, and your child searches.

What you need

  • a curtain at a closed window

Safety

The window is closed with no cords nearby; nobody pulls on or hangs from the curtain.

If your child gets scared, peek out straight away and give them a hug.

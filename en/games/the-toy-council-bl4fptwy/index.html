The toy council

4-6 yearsemotionsno mess18 min

Act out an argument with the toys while your child, as chief adviser, names the characters’ feelings and looks for a solution.

Setup

Sit 2 soft toys around a cushion: this is the council table. Today your child is the chief adviser.

Steps

  1. Act out an argument in different voices: Teddy and Bunny both want the same car and both say “Mine.”
  2. Ask how each of them feels. Let your child name the feelings and suggest how the friends can make up.
  3. Act out each suggestion with the toys: give the car away, take turns, find a second car.
  4. Your child announces the fairest solution and the toys make up. Then your child invents a new argument and you are the adviser.

What you need

  • 2 soft toys
  • a toy car
  • a cushion

Safety

If the story upsets your child, switch to a made-up argument or end the game.

Melting ice figures

2-3 yearsthinking35 min

Melt a block of ice together with warm water and a pinch of salt, and dig out the figures hidden inside.

Steps

  1. Pour warm water over the ice with the spoon: a little dent appears.

What you need

  • a large container for freezing
  • 3 plastic figures
  • a tray with raised edges
  • a bowl of warm water
  • a spoon
  • salt

Safety

Sprinkle the salt yourself: it’s not for eating, and wet hands shouldn’t touch eyes.

Keep the water warm, not hot, and stay within arm’s reach the whole time.

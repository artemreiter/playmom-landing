Fluffy foam for little hands

15-18 monthssenses15 min

Whip up foam from chickpea liquid and play with it together: scoop it, spoon it around and draw in it with a finger.

Steps

  1. Scoop up some foam in your palm and blow it sideways: “Whoosh, off it goes.”

What you need

  • a tin of chickpeas
  • a mixer
  • a tray with raised edges
  • a spoon
  • a plastic cup

Safety

The foam is only chickpea liquid, with no shampoo or dye, so it’s fine if it goes in their mouth.

If your baby is allergic to legumes, pour a little warm water instead of foam and stay within arm’s reach.

Wipe up any spilled foam straight away.

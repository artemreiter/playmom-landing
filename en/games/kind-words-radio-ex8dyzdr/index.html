Kind words radio

4-7 yearsemotionsno prepno mess16 min

Your child records a short, kind message for everyone in the family on a voice memo app, then plays them on air at dinner.

Setup

Open the voice memo app on your phone and show your child the record button.

Steps

  1. Tell your child they’re the host of “Radio of Kind Words” and will record one message for each person in the family.
  2. Let your child choose who gets the first message, starting with “Thank you for” or “I love it when you.”
  3. Don’t feed them the words: let them say it however it comes out.
  4. At dinner, your child announces the show and plays the messages one by one, and each listener answers with a kind word of their own.

What you need

  • a phone with a voice memo app

Safety

The recordings stay on the phone: they only go into a chat or on social media with your child’s permission.

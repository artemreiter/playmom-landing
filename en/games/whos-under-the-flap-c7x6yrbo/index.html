Who’s under the flap

12-15 monthsspeechno messbedtime8 min

Open the flaps of a board book together, name who’s hiding there and wait to see which flap your baby picks.

Setup

Take a sturdy lift-the-flap board book and sit with your baby so they can see the page.

Steps

  1. Point to a flap and ask: “Who’s there?” Help your baby lift it.
  2. Name the picture and make its sound: “A cat. Miaow.”
  3. On the next page, wait: let your baby pull the flap themselves, then you name who they found.
  4. Close the flap and ask: “Where’s the cat?” Let your baby choose which flap to open again.

What you need

  • a lift-the-flap board book

Safety

Use a sturdy board book; if a flap comes off, take it away at once.

Stop when your baby loses interest or pushes the book away.

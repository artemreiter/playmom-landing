Autumn leaves dance

2-4 yearsmovementno mess14 min

Dance to music with light scarves: swirl like leaves in a strong wind, then sink down softly to the floor.

Steps

  1. Give your child a scarf for each hand, and take 2 yourself: these are the autumn leaves.

What you need

  • 4 light scarves
  • a phone with music

Safety

Keep the scarves in your hands only: never tie them around the neck or drape them over the head.

Twirl on the rug, well away from the corners of furniture.

Fuzzy creature hunt

24-30 monthsgross motor skillsno mess16 min

Hide sock fuzzies under cushions and a blanket on the floor, then let your little one crawl through the jungle and scoop them into a basket.

Steps

  1. Say that fuzzies have snuck into the jungle, then squeak softly near one hiding spot.

What you need

  • 5 pairs of socks
  • sofa cushions
  • a blanket
  • a basket

Safety

Keep the cushions and blanket on the floor, and don’t let your little one climb onto the sofa or table.

Never cover your little one’s head with the blanket.

Happy finger, sleepy finger

6-9 monthsemotionsno prepno mess5 min

Bring your finger to life: let it dance happily, fall asleep in your fist and wake up again for your baby.

Setup

Sit or lie down next to your baby so they can see your hands clearly.

Steps

  1. Raise your index finger and say cheerfully: “Hi, I’m Little Finger.” Let it dance across your baby’s palm.
  2. Slowly hide the finger in your fist and say softly: “Little Finger is tired. Sleeping.”
  3. Wait a couple of seconds and show it again: “Awake.” Repeat if your baby smiles or reaches out.
  4. When your baby grabs the finger, let it say a happy hello and shake their little hand.

Safety

Move your hands gently, with no sudden movements near your baby’s face.

Stop if your baby turns away or seems tired.

Shadow puppets on the wall

21-24 monthsspeechno messbedtime32 min

Act out a short story about a cat and a bird with shadows on the wall, and your baby moves the bird and speaks for it.

Setup

Cut a cat and a bird bigger than your palm out of thick cardboard. Draw the curtains, leaving the room dim.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch on the wall and move the cat through the beam: “Miaow, here comes the cat.”
  2. Act out the story: the cat sleeps, the bird flies in, the cat wakes up: “Miaow.”
  3. Give your baby the bird: let them decide where it flies and tweet for it, “Tweet tweet.”
  4. Play it again, and your baby chooses who sleeps this time: the cat or the bird.

What you need

  • a torch
  • thick cardboard
  • scissors

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down cover, and don’t shine it in your baby’s eyes.

No sticks on the figures; put the scissors away after cutting.

Kitchen of sounds

18-21 monthsspeechno mess19 min

Give the dishes and paper funny names based on their sound, take turns playing them, and guess who’s sounding off behind your back.

Steps

  1. Play each one in turn and name it: “This is Rustle, and it says shh-shh.” Let your baby try it too.

What you need

  • a metal bowl
  • a whisk
  • a sheet of paper
  • 2 wooden spoons

Safety

Leave out the grater, foil and a rice-filled bottle, and use only unbreakable dishes.

If your baby puts the paper in their mouth, take it away and pick up any torn scraps.

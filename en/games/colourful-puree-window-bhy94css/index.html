Colourful puree window

12-15 monthsemotions17 min

Smear baby puree together across a window, look outside through the colourful splotches, and wash the glass clean with a damp cloth.

Steps

  1. Dab 3 blobs of puree onto the glass at your baby’s eye level and name the colours.

What you need

  • 2 to 3 baby purees in different colours
  • a saucer
  • a towel
  • a damp cloth

Safety

The puree is safe to taste: use only flavours your baby has already had without any reaction.

Keep the window closed, and have your baby stand on the floor, not on the windowsill.

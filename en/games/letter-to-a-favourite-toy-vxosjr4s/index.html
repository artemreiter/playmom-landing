Letter to a favourite toy

3-4 yearsspeechno messbedtime18 min

Your child draws a letter to a favourite toy and dictates a line or two for you to write, then finds a reply from the toy in the morning.

Setup

Put a sheet of paper, coloured pencils and an envelope on the table, and sit your child’s favourite toy nearby.

Steps

  1. Say that toys read their letters at night, and ask which toy your child wants to write to.
  2. Your child draws what happened today, while you write a line or two at the bottom as they dictate.
  3. Read the letter out loud together, then your child puts it in the envelope and leaves it by the toy at bedtime.
  4. Once your child is asleep, write a short reply with a paw print, and read it out loud in the morning.

What you need

  • a sheet of paper
  • coloured pencils
  • an envelope
  • a favourite soft toy

Safety

Don’t seal the envelope by licking it, just tuck the flap inside.

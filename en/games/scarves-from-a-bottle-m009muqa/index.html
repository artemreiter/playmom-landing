Scarves from a bottle

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno mess15 min

Push thin scarves into a bottle, and your baby pulls them out by the corners like a magician.

Setup

Push three thin scarves in different fabrics into a wide-necked bottle, leaving the corners outside.

Steps

  1. Hold the bottle in front of your baby and slowly pull out one scarf: “Pu-u-ull.”
  2. Bring the next corner up to your baby’s palm and wait for them to pull.
  3. Name the scarf while your baby pulls it: “Smooth,” “Soft.”
  4. Push the scarves back in: whichever corner your baby grabs, pull that one out together.

What you need

  • a wide-necked plastic bottle
  • 3 thin scarves

Safety

Scarves only: no ribbons, cords, dried peas or small bits on the ends.

Don’t drape the scarves over your baby’s face or head, and put them away after the game.

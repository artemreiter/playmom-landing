What the stones whisper

4-7 yearsattentionno messbedtime23 min

Draw simple symbols on stones, then whisper a sequence of symbols for your child to lay the stones out in the same order.

Steps

  1. Let your child think up five simple symbols, such as a sun, a star and a wave, and draw them on the stones with a marker.

What you need

  • 5 smooth, palm-sized stones
  • a permanent marker

Safety

Keep the marker with you and let the symbols dry before your child picks up the stones; no throwing stones.

Catch the sparkling fireflies

3-4 yearsgross motor skillsno mess15 min

Move the flashlight beam over the walls and floor of a dim room while your child catches the firefly with a hand or foot.

Steps

  1. Shine the flashlight on the wall: “That's a sparkling firefly.” Let your child catch the spot with a palm.

What you need

  • a flashlight with a screwed battery cover
  • a room with curtains

Safety

Only you hold the flashlight with a screwed battery cover, and never point it at eyes.

Catch it by walking, no running in the dim light; turn the light on if your child feels unsure.

Singing porridge

24-30 monthsspeechno mess16 min

Cook up a pretend pot of porridge and give every step a sound: shhh, blub-blub, plop, knock-knock.

Setup

Put a small pot, a spoon, an empty bowl and cup, three big blocks and a teddy bear on the floor.

Steps

  1. Pour pretend oats from the empty bowl: “Shhh.” Let your baby copy the sound.
  2. Your baby pours pretend water from the empty cup: “Blub-blub,” and stirs it with the spoon: “Round and round.”
  3. Your baby drops the blocks into the pot like raisins, saying “Plop” together for each one.
  4. Tap the spoon on the rim: “Knock-knock, it’s ready,” and let your baby feed the teddy bear.
  5. Swap roles: your baby cooks the porridge for you and picks the sounds, while you copy them.

What you need

  • a small pot
  • a wooden spoon
  • an empty bowl and cup
  • three big blocks
  • a soft teddy bear

Safety

The oats, raisins and water are all make-believe, and the blocks and teddy bear are bigger than your baby’s fist.

Surprise dough

18-21 monthsfine motor skills25 min

Wrap large toys in homemade dough, and your baby squashes it and finds out who’s hiding inside.

Steps

  1. Give your baby a piece of dough to play with: squeeze it, pat it, pull off a little lump.

What you need

  • flour
  • water
  • vegetable oil
  • 2 large toys
  • a wipe-clean tablecloth

Safety

Only large toys in the dough: no buttons, pasta or small figures.

No salt or food colouring in the dough; if your baby puts it in their mouth, take the piece away.

If there’s a wheat allergy, use cornflour instead of flour.

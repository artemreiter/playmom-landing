Touch and feel book

21-24 monthsemotionsno messbedtime37 min

Make a little book from cardboard pages covered in fuzzy fabric and denim, and explore together how each page feels.

Setup

Cut 3 postcard-sized pieces of cardboard from a box, glue fuzzy terry fabric onto one, denim onto another, and leave the third smooth. Bind them together along one edge with tape.

Steps

  1. Flip through the book together and pet each page: “Fuzzy,” “Rough,” “Smooth.”
  2. Let your baby turn the pages themselves and choose which one to pet again.
  3. Ask: “Where’s the fuzzy one?” Wait for your baby to find the page, and pet it together.
  4. Swap roles: your baby points to a page, and you describe how it feels.

What you need

  • a cardboard box
  • a piece of terry fabric
  • a piece of denim fabric
  • glue
  • wide tape
  • scissors

Safety

Leave out string, buttons, plastic film and foil, and keep the glue and scissors to yourself.

If the fabric or tape comes loose, put the book away and reglue it.

Box towers

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno mess27 min

Build towers and walls from light empty boxes with your baby, then let your baby knock them down with a crash.

Steps

  1. Put two boxes on top of each other: “Tower.” Invite your baby to add a third.

What you need

  • 5 empty shoebox-sized boxes

Safety

Only light empty boxes with no staples, and the tower no higher than your baby’s chest.

Nobody climbs or stands on the boxes.

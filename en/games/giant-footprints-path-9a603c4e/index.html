Giant footprints path

21-24 monthsgross motor skillsno mess22 min

Lay big footprints drawn on paper across the rug and walk the path with your child, making up who left them.

Steps

  1. Stand on the first footprint and stomp: “A giant walked here.” Call your child to stand next to you.

What you need

  • 6 sheets of heavy paper
  • a marker
  • a rug

Safety

Put the sheets only on the rug, no further than your child's step: paper slides on a smooth floor.

Keep the marker to yourself.

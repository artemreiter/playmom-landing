Crossing cushion islands

24-30 monthsmovementno mess23 min

The floor turns into a river: your child steps across cushion islands and carries a soft toy to the other bank.

Steps

  1. Say: “The floor is a river now, and you can only walk on the islands.” Cross the first few islands yourself.

What you need

  • 6 to 8 cushions and folded towels
  • a soft toy

Safety

Keep the cushions on the rug only, never stacked, and stay away from furniture corners.

Step, don’t jump: that way the cushions won’t slide out from underfoot.

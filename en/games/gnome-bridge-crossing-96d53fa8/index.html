Gnome bridge crossing

3-5 yearsmovementno mess19 min

Your child becomes a gnome and crosses the chasm only by stepping on cushion-and-towel stones, then builds a bridge for you to cross.

Steps

  1. Say: “You’re a gnome, the floor is a chasm, and the only way to the house is across the stones.”

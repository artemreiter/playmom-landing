Dance along the paper thread

2-4 yearsmovement16 min

Unroll toilet paper into a winding path, and your child walks along it to teddy, making up ways to step.

Steps

  1. Say: “This is a magic thread, it leads to teddy.” Walk along it first, stepping on the paper.

Shimmering bottles

6-9 monthssensesno mess22 min

Rock and roll two sealed bottles of water with your baby and look at the bubbles and shimmer by the window.

Setup

Half fill two small plastic bottles with water and add a spoonful of vegetable oil to one. Screw the caps on and wrap them in several layers of wide tape.

Steps

  1. Sit with your baby by the window. Rock a bottle: “Glug, glug,” and give it to your baby.
  2. Turn the bottle with oil upside down: watch together as the bubbles slowly creep along.
  3. Lay a bottle on the floor and gently roll it to your baby; let them push it back, or nudge their hand yourself.
  4. Put both bottles side by side: whichever one your baby picks up, roll that one next.

What you need

  • two small plastic bottles with caps
  • water
  • vegetable oil
  • wide packing tape

Safety

Before playing, check that nothing leaks; if the tape is peeling or a bottle is cracked, put it away.

No glitter, beads or buttons inside.

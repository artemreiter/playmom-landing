Caterpillar crawls to the leaf

24-30 monthsgross motor skillsno prepno mess8 min

Crawl on your tummy across the rug like caterpillars towards a toy leaf and take turns choosing where to put it.

Setup

Put any soft toy on the rug 3 steps away, this is the leaf.

Steps

  1. Lie on your tummy and crawl to the leaf, pulling yourself along with your arms: “I’m a caterpillar, I want the leaf.”
  2. Let your baby lie on their tummy beside you and crawl to the leaf however they can: with their arms, or helping with their legs.
  3. Once your baby reaches it, let them “eat” the leaf: “Yum, yum.”
  4. Now your baby puts the leaf in a new spot, and you’re the one crawling as the caterpillar.

What you need

  • a rug
  • a soft toy

Safety

Don’t hold your baby’s legs or lift them up: the caterpillar crawls on its tummy on its own.

Clear small objects off the path and crawl well away from furniture corners.

Box of scarves

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno mess17 min

Tuck scarves into an empty tissue box, and your baby tugs a corner and pulls them out one by one.

Steps

  1. Put the box in front of your baby and wiggle the corner: “What’s in there?”

What you need

  • an empty tissue box
  • 3-4 light scarves

Safety

Don’t tie the scarves together or drape them over your baby’s face or head; no bells or threads are needed.

Use a box with no staples, and put the scarves away after playing.

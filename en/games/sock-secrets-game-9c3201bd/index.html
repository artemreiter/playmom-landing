Sock secrets game

9-12 monthssensesno mess12 min

Feel 3 socks together, each with a different secret inside, something soft, something rustly and something hard, then pull out what’s hidden.

Steps

  1. Give your baby the sock with the towel and squeeze it together: “Soft.”

What you need

  • 3 large socks
  • a small towel
  • a sheet of baking paper
  • a wooden spoon

Safety

Don’t put grains, cotton wool or small items into the socks, and tie a knot in the sock itself instead of using an elastic band.

If your baby pulls the paper out, take it back and put it in the sock again.

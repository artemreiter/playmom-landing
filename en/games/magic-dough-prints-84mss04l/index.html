Magic dough prints

15-18 monthsattention24 min

Roll out soft, salt-free dough and press prints into it with a cup, a toy car and your baby’s palm, while your baby picks what to press next.

Steps

  1. Press the bottom of a cup into the dough and show your baby the round print it leaves.

What you need

  • flour, water and vegetable oil
  • a rimmed tray
  • a rolling pin
  • a plastic cup
  • a big toy car

Safety

The cup and the car are bigger than your baby’s fist.

Raw dough isn’t for eating: if your baby tries to put it in their mouth, take the piece away.

If there’s a wheat allergy, use rice flour instead.

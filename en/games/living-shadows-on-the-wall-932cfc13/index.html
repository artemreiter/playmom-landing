Shine a torch on the wall and let your child move objects through the beam, turn the shadows into creatures and make up a story about them.

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover, and never shine it in anyone’s eyes.

If the half-light feels unsettling, turn the lights back on.

Digging in paper snowdrifts

18-21 monthsattentionno mess23 min

Hide large toys under balls of paper in a box, and your child digs them out, names them and buries them for you.

Setup

Scrunch up 10-15 whole sheets of paper and tip them into a large box or washing-up bowl. Hide 3-4 toys bigger than a fist underneath.

Steps

  1. Reach into the paper, pull out a toy and act surprised: “Wow, who’s hiding here?”
  2. Let your child dig with their hands until they find the next toy.
  3. Name each find with its sound and line it up: “A cow. Moo.” Count the finds out loud.
  4. Swap: your child buries the toys while you search and ask: “Here?”

What you need

  • a large box or washing-up bowl
  • 10-15 sheets of paper
  • 3-4 toys bigger than a fist

Safety

Don’t use rice, beans or small figures: the toys are bigger than your child’s fist.

If the paper tears, gather the scraps after each round; the box has no staples or sharp edges.

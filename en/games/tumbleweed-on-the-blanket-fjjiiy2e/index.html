Tumbleweed on the blanket

6-9 monthsgross motor skillsno mess12 min

Tuck rolled-up duvets under a blanket, and your baby crawls over the low hills to a toy or rolls over with your help.

Steps

  1. Lay your baby on their tummy in front of a hill and show a toy behind it: “Where’s the ball?”

What you need

  • a blanket
  • one or two duvets
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Don’t pile up cushions: your baby could bury their face in something soft.

Stay within arm’s reach and turn your baby onto their back when they get tired.

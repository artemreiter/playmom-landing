Tea towel safari

9-12 monthsfine motor skillsno mess12 min

Sit toy animals on the edge of a tea towel, and your baby pulls it towards them so the animals ride over.

Setup

Spread a tea towel on the floor. On the far edge, sit 2-3 toy animals bigger than your baby’s fist.

Steps

  1. Sit next to your baby and put the near edge of the towel by their hands.
  2. Slowly pull the towel yourself: the animals ride towards your baby. “The elephant’s here.”
  3. Sit the animals on the far edge again and wait for your baby to pull the towel themselves.
  4. Let your baby choose who rides next: the animal they reach for gets on the towel.

What you need

  • a tea towel
  • 2-3 toy animals

Safety

Use animals bigger than your baby’s fist, with no small parts; don’t use paper clips or magnets.

Don’t drape the towel over your baby’s face or head.

Calling storybook characters

30-36 monthsspeechno prepno mess14 min

Your toddler calls the Gingerbread Man and a bear, while you answer in their voices and ask for advice on what to do next.

Setup

Nothing to prepare.

Steps

  1. Hand your toddler a block or toy phone, and let them choose who to call: the Gingerbread Man or the bear.
  2. Hold your hand to your ear and answer in the character’s voice: “Hello, this is the Gingerbread Man, I’m rolling down the path.”
  3. Ask for advice: “The fox wants me to sit on her nose. Should I?” and listen to your toddler’s answer.
  4. Call again as the bear: “Someone ate my porridge, what should I do?” Let your toddler comfort him or offer advice.
  5. Swap roles: your toddler speaks for the character, and you make the call and ask a question.

What you need

  • a battery-free toy phone or a building block

Safety

Don’t give your toddler a real phone or remote control, since they have a battery cover that opens.

An echo cave in a box

15-18 monthsspeechno mess13 min

Your toddler climbs into a big box cave, and you take turns drawing out sounds and knocking to hear them echo inside.

Setup

Lay a large cardboard box with no staples or sharp edges on its side on the rug.

Steps

  1. Help your toddler climb into the box and sit at the entrance facing them.
  2. Draw out a quiet “Aaah” or “Ooh” and wait for an answering sound.
  3. Echo your toddler’s sound back, a little quieter each time.
  4. Tap the side of the box with your palm, then let your toddler tap it too.
  5. Let your toddler call a toy into the cave, and you answer for it from outside.

What you need

  • a large cardboard box
  • a soft toy

Safety

Don’t cover the box with anything or close its flaps, and stay at the entrance.

Stop if your toddler seems scared or it gets too loud.

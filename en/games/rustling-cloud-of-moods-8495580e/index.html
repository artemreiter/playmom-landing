Rustling cloud of moods

6-9 monthsemotionsno prepno mess4 min

Rustle a sheet of paper quietly, loudly and playfully while changing your expression, and your child listens, crumples it and rustles back.

Setup

Sit facing your child and take a sheet of clean heavy unprinted paper or a piece of rustly fabric.

Steps

  1. Rustle the sheet slowly and softly, make a thoughtful face and say quietly: “Shhh.”
  2. Rustle faster with a surprised face: “Oh.” Then rustle playfully with a smile: “Hee hee.”
  3. Let your child crumple the sheet or fabric. Repeat whichever sound and face they responded to.
  4. Wait for your child to rustle it themselves, and answer with the same kind of rustle.

What you need

  • a sheet of clean heavy paper or a rustly piece of fabric

Safety

Rustle quietly and stop if your child flinches or turns away.

If a piece tears off the sheet, take it away right away and give a whole sheet or the fabric instead.

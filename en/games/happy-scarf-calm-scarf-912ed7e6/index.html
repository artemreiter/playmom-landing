Happy scarf, calm scarf

6-9 monthsemotionsno mess7 min

Move a scarf slowly and quietly, then fast and playfully, and watch which one your baby responds to more.

Setup

Lay your baby on their back or sit them on a play mat. Take one light scarf.

Steps

  1. Slowly sweep the scarf above your baby and over their palm, saying softly: “Calm scarf, shhh.”
  2. Shake the scarf quickly beside your baby: “Happy scarf, whoosh.”
  3. Put the scarf on your head. When your baby reaches for it, let them pull it off: “Peekaboo.”
  4. Repeat whichever version your baby answered with a smile, a sound or a hand movement.

What you need

  • one light scarf

Safety

Don’t cover your baby’s face with the scarf or leave the fabric with them unattended.

Stop if your baby turns away or starts to fuss.

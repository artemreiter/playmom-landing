Secret pictures for a detective

24-30 monthsthinking20 min

Your child paints over a sheet with coloured water and finds a picture hidden in white crayon, then hides their own.

Setup

Draw a big circle, house or wave on white paper with a white wax crayon. Pour a little water into a bowl and add a drop of food colouring.

Steps

  1. Say: “There’s a secret hidden here. Let’s find it with the brush.”
  2. Dip the brush in the coloured water yourself and hand it to your child. Let them paint wherever they like.
  3. When the white line shows through, ask: “What’s that? A circle or a house?”
  4. Swap: your child scribbles with the white crayon, and you reveal it with the brush.

What you need

  • white paper
  • a white wax crayon
  • a paintbrush
  • a bowl
  • water
  • food colouring

Safety

Keep the bowl on your side, only you add the colouring, and use just a little water. Stay within arm’s reach.

If your child puts the brush or crayon in their mouth, take them away.

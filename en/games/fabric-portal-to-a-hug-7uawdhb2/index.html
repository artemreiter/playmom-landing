Fabric portal to a hug

12-15 monthsmovementno mess15 min

Hold a piece of fabric up in front of you like a curtain, and let your little one crawl up, pull it aside, and find you waiting with a hug.

Setup

Spread a soft mat well away from furniture, and set a terry towel, a thin scarf and a swaddle blanket nearby.

Steps

  1. Sit on the mat 2 steps from your little one and lift the towel up in front of you like a curtain.
  2. Call out: “Where am I? Come here,” and wait for your little one to crawl over and tug at the fabric.
  3. When the fabric slides aside, reveal yourself with a smile and hug your little one: “Here I am.”
  4. Let your little one stroke the fabric, show them the other 2, and hide behind whichever one they reach for next.

What you need

  • a terry towel
  • a thin scarf
  • a swaddle blanket
  • a soft mat

Safety

Only you hold the fabric, and never drape it over your little one’s face or head.

No string, chairs or clips are needed.

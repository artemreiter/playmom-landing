Sparkly droplets

24-30 monthssenses13 min

Spray water into a torch beam and watch the droplets sparkle, while your child chooses where to shine it.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch over the towel and spray water into the beam: “Look, sparkles.”

What you need

  • a new spray bottle
  • water
  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a towel

Safety

Clean water only, nothing scented. Hold the torch with the screwed-down cover yourself and don’t shine it in anyone’s eyes.

Stay within arm’s reach and wipe up a wet floor straight away.

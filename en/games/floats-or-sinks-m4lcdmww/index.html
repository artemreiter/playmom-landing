Floats or sinks

6-9 monthssenses12 min

Dip a rubber duck and a spoon into a basin of warm water and see with your baby what floats and what sinks.

Steps

  1. Drop the rubber duck into the water and rock it: “It floats.”

What you need

  • a stable basin
  • warm water
  • a towel
  • a rubber duck
  • a tablespoon
  • a flannel

Safety

Check the water with your elbow and stay within arm’s reach the whole time.

If you need to step away, take your baby with you and empty the water out.

Don’t use stones, sponges or bottles with lids.

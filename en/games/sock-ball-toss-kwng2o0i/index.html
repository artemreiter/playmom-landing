Sock ball toss

18-21 monthsgross motor skillsno mess22 min

Take turns tossing sock balls into a basin and a box, while your baby picks the target and gathers the balls for the next round.

Steps

  1. Stand next to your baby and underhand-toss a ball into the basin: “Got it.”

What you need

  • 5 pairs of socks
  • a basin
  • a cardboard box

Safety

Don’t fill the socks with rice or beans: the balls are made from socks alone.

Keep the basin and box empty and dry, and make sure the box has no staples or sharp edges.

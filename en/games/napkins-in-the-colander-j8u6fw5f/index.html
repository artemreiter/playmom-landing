Napkins in the colander

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno messbedtime11 min

Pull cloth napkins out of a colander by their dangling corners together and tuck them back in.

Steps

  1. Pull a corner and slowly draw out the napkin: “Ooh, what’s this?”

What you need

  • a plastic colander
  • 3 cloth napkins

Safety

Instead of ribbons, use only small napkins with no fringe, threads or ties.

Don’t drape a napkin over your baby’s face, and put the napkins away after the game.

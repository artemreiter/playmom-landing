Calm-down bottle

2-5 yearsemotions20 min

Your child pours the water and picks the glitter, then shakes the bottle and breathes slowly while the glitter settles.

Steps

  1. Hold the bottle steady while your child pours water in up to halfway.

What you need

  • a clear 0.5 litre plastic bottle with a lid
  • a small jug of water
  • glitter
  • wide packing tape
  • a tray

Safety

While your child pours, stay within arm’s reach and wipe up puddles straight away.

No beads, soap, food colouring or glue are needed; put a cracked bottle away at once.

Keep the finished bottle yourself and don’t leave your child alone with it.

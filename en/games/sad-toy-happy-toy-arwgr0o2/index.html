Sad toy, happy toy

9-12 monthsemotionsno prepno mess6 min

Show sadness, joy and surprise with a soft toy while your baby strokes it and chooses what to repeat.

Setup

No preparation needed. Sit your baby facing you and take a soft toy or doll.

Steps

  1. Tilt the toy and say in a sad voice: “Oh dear, I’m sad.” Wait to see what your baby does.
  2. Show how to stroke the toy and bring it close to your baby. After a stroke, the toy cheers up: “Hooray.”
  3. Toss the toy up a little in your hands and look surprised: “Oh.”
  4. Repeat whatever your baby responded to with a smile or a movement. Then give them the toy.

What you need

  • a soft toy or doll

Safety

Use a toy with no buttons or small parts. Stop if your baby turns away or seems tired.

Catching little clouds

24-30 monthsattentionno mess15 min

Drop a napkin and a scarf from up high, and your child catches the slowly falling clouds, then launches them for you.

Steps

  1. Lift the napkin up high and let it go: “Here comes a cloud.” Let your child catch it with their hands.

What you need

  • a paper napkin
  • a light scarf

Safety

Don’t use cotton wool, pompoms or balls of paper, only a whole napkin and the scarf.

Don’t drop the scarf onto your child’s face; if it lands on their head, lift it off straight away.

Colour potions

24-30 monthsindependence22 min

Mix coloured water together in clear cups and watch what colour the new potion turns out.

Setup

Put three clear plastic cups on a tray and pour a little water into two of them. Add yellow food colouring to one and blue to the other.

Steps

  1. Show the two potions and ask which one your child wants to pour first.
  2. Let your child pour a little of the chosen potion into the empty cup and stir it with the spoon.
  3. Add the second colour and look together at what you made: “A green potion.”
  4. Swap: your child mixes however they like, and you guess what colour it will be.

What you need

  • a tray
  • three clear plastic cups
  • water
  • yellow and blue food colouring
  • a teaspoon
  • a towel

Safety

Pour only a little water into each cup and stay within arm’s reach. Only you add the food colouring.

If your child puts a cup in their mouth, take it away; wipe up spills straight away.

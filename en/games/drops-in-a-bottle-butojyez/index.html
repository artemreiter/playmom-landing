Drops in a bottle

9-12 monthsattentionno mess16 min

Tip and roll a bottle of water and oil together, and your baby watches the yellow drops race upwards.

Setup

Fill a plastic bottle a third full with vegetable oil, top it up with water almost to the top, screw the lid on tight and tape over it.

Steps

  1. Sit by a window and slowly turn the bottle over: the oil drops will float upwards.
  2. Say “They’re floating” and wait together while the drops gather at the top.
  3. Roll the bottle across the floor to your baby so they can push it back.
  4. Let your baby choose: turn the bottle over together or roll it again.

What you need

  • a clear 0.5 litre plastic bottle
  • vegetable oil
  • water

Safety

Before playing, check the lid is taped and the bottle doesn’t leak; if it does, put it away.

No fizzy tablets, food colouring or glue; stay within arm’s reach.

If your baby puts the bottle or the lid in their mouth, take it away.

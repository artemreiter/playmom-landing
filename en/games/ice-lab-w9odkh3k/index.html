Ice lab

30-36 monthsthinking35 min

Your child drips warm water from a pipette while you sprinkle salt, and the toys escape one by one from the block of ice.

Setup

The day before, freeze 3 large toys in a container, adding the water in layers. Tip the ice onto a tray, with a pipette and a bowl of shallow warm water beside it.

Steps

  1. Drip warm water onto the ice with the pipette: a little hollow appears. Give your child the pipette.
  2. Let your child choose where to dig and drip the water themselves.
  3. Your child points to a spot, you sprinkle a pinch of salt on it: the ice crackles and melts faster.
  4. When a toy appears, your child pulls it out with their fingers and names it. Then they pick the next spot.

What you need

  • a container for freezing
  • 3 large toys
  • a tray with raised edges
  • a bowl of warm water
  • a pipette
  • salt

Safety

The water is warm to the touch and shallow; stay within arm’s reach.

Keep the salt with you. Freeze only toys bigger than your child’s fist, with no buttons, beads or coins.

Straw lacing shoe

24-36 monthsfine motor skillsno mess27 min

Thread drinking straws through the holes of a cardboard shoe together: in through one hole, out through the next.

Setup

Cut a big shoe shape, about the size of a notebook page, out of corrugated cardboard, and poke 6 holes around the edge with a pencil. Put the scissors and pencil away before playing.

Steps

  1. Put a straw into a hole, turn the cardboard over and pull it through the other side: “It’s through.”
  2. Hold the cardboard while your child threads a straw through any hole.
  3. Suggest pulling the straw out and threading it into the next hole. Don’t correct any holes they skip.
  4. Once every hole has a straw sticking out of it, your child picks which one to pull out first, and it all starts again.

What you need

  • corrugated cardboard
  • scissors
  • a pencil
  • 3 plastic drinking straws

Safety

Check the edges of the cardboard and trim off any sharp corners before playing.

Play sitting at a table, and swap out any straw right away if it gets chewed.

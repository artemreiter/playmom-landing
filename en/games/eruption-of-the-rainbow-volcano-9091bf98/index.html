Eruption of the rainbow volcano

3-4 yearsfine motor skills23 min

Your child drips water from a pipette onto the top of a paper volcano, and the painted lava spreads down the sides in different colours.

Setup

Crumple three sheets of kitchen roll into a mound on a tray, cover with a fourth sheet, draw bright spots on top with felt-tip pens, and pour a little water into a bowl.

Steps

  1. Drip from the pipette onto the top: “The volcano is waking up.”
  2. Let your child drip onto the spots themselves and watch the lava crawl down the sides.
  3. Ask which colour to add, and draw a spot wherever your child points.
  4. Take turns dripping until the volcano turns into a rainbow.

What you need

  • kitchen roll
  • washable felt-tip pens
  • a plastic pipette
  • a bowl
  • a tray
  • water

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach: there’s only a little clean water in the bowl, and the felt-tip pens stay with you.

Don’t let wet paper go in the mouth, and wipe up any spills right away.

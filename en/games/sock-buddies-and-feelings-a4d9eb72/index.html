Sock buddies and feelings

30-36 monthsemotionsno prepno mess30 min

Put a clean sock on each hand and act out scenes with a happy, sad and surprised buddy together with your child.

Setup

Take two clean socks: put one on your own hand and one on your child's. These are the buddies.

Steps

  1. The happy buddy jumps and laughs: “Ha ha.” Make a happy face and let your child copy it.
  2. The sad buddy lowers its head and sighs, the surprised one says “Oh”. Your child copies the faces.
  3. Act it out: “The happy buddy met the sad one. How can we help?” Let your child's buddy answer.
  4. Your child picks their buddy's mood while yours guesses it and responds.

What you need

  • 2 clean socks

Safety

No scissors, glue or thread needed: the socks are clean, have no buttons and only go on a hand.

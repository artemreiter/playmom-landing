Toy under the scarf

6-9 monthsattentionno mess5 min

Hide a favourite toy under a light scarf, pause, then uncover it or let your baby pull the scarf off.

Steps

  1. Show the toy and slowly cover it with the scarf: “Where’s the toy? It’s hiding.”

What you need

  • a light scarf
  • two of your baby’s toys

Safety

The scarf covers only the toy, never your baby’s face or head.

Put the scarf away after the game; your baby doesn’t play with it without you.

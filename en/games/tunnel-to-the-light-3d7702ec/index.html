Tunnel to the light

18-21 monthsmovementno mess19 min

Your baby crawls under a table covered with a sheet towards the torch light, then chooses which end to crawl from next.

Setup

Drape a sheet over the dining table so that both ends stay open.

Steps

  1. Peek into the tunnel together: “A cave. Let’s crawl to the light.”
  2. Sit at the other end, shine the torch on the floor in front of you and call your baby.
  3. When your baby crawls out, give them a hug and switch off the light: “You made it.”
  4. Ask where to crawl from now, and take the light to wherever your baby points.
  5. Swap: your baby waits at the exit and you crawl.

What you need

  • a dining table
  • a sheet
  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover; shine it at the floor, not in anyone’s eyes.

Make the tunnel only under a sturdy table, with no chairs; both ends open, nothing inside.

What you need

  • 2 soft toys
  • bowls, spoons and empty containers
  • paper to scrunch into balls
  • fabric scraps

Safety

Instead of grains, beans, pasta and pebbles, use paper balls and fabric scraps bigger than your child’s palm. Food is only tasted for pretend.

Patterns of light on the wall

24-30 monthssensesno mess17 min

Shine a torch on the wall, and let your toddler hold a colander, a cup and a bottle of water in the beam to see what patterns appear.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch on the wall and hold the colander up to the beam: “Look, little stars.”

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a colander
  • a coloured plastic cup
  • a plastic bottle of water
  • tape

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover, and never shine it in anyone’s eyes.

Take the taped bottle with you whenever you step away.

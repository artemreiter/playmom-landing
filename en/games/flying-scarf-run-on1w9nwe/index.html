Flying scarf run

21-24 monthsgross motor skillsno mess27 min

Run together holding scarves up high: they fly behind you as you run, and droop down when you stop.

Setup

Grab 2 light scarves with no fringe, one for you and one for your child, and head to a long hallway or an open flat space.

Steps

  1. Blow on your scarf: the edge flutters. Let your child blow on theirs.
  2. Hold your child’s hand and run a few steps, holding the scarves by a corner: they fly out behind you.
  3. Stop: the scarves droop down. Walk slowly, then run faster, and notice when the scarf flies higher.
  4. Swap roles: your child calls out “Run” and “Stop,” and you run with the scarf.

What you need

  • 2 light scarves

Safety

Don’t tie the scarf on or drape it over the face.

Run only on flat, even flooring, away from steps and furniture corners.

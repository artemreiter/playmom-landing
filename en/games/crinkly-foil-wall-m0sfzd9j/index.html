Crinkly foil wall

9-12 monthssensesno mess15 min

Tape foil to the wall, and your baby bangs on it, scrunches it with a palm and looks at their blurry reflection.

Steps

  1. Tap the foil with your palm: “Bang, it rustles.” Wait for your baby to reach out.

What you need

  • a roll of foil
  • masking tape
  • a piece of soft fabric

Safety

Don’t tear the foil. Remove any torn-off bit at once, or your baby may put it in their mouth.

No cotton wool, sandpaper or yoghurt are needed; stay close the whole time.

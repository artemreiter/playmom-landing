Cushion obstacle crawl

9-12 monthsmovementno mess9 min

Lay a row of sofa cushions on the rug, and let your baby crawl over them to reach a favourite toy.

Steps

  1. Sit behind the cushions, show the toy, and call your baby over.

What you need

  • three firm sofa cushions
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Use only firm sofa cushions in a single layer, never sleep pillows.

Stay close and pick your baby up right away if their face presses into a cushion.

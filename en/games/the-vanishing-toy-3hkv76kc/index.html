The vanishing toy

9-18 monthsattentionno prepno mess6 min

Hide a toy under a scarf or behind your back with an “Abracadabra” and let your baby find it.

Steps

  1. Show the toy, cover it with the scarf and say: “Abracadabra, where did it go?”

What you need

  • a toy bigger than your baby’s fist

Safety

The toy is bigger than your baby’s fist and has no small parts.

Don’t cover your baby’s face with the scarf.

Dress the toys for the weather

24-30 monthsindependenceno mess20 min

Pick the weather and dress teddy in old children’s clothes, then put on a little show and swap roles.

Setup

Put some old children’s socks, a hat and a couple of scarves in a box. Sit two soft toys nearby.

Steps

  1. Say: “It’s raining outside. What shall teddy wear?” Let your child pick something from the box.
  2. Help pull a sock on as a hat or drape a scarf as a cape.
  3. Show off teddy’s outfit: “Teddy’s going for a walk.” Clap together.
  4. Swap: your child picks the weather, sun or snow, and you dress the second toy.

What you need

  • old children’s socks
  • a child’s hat
  • two scarves
  • two soft toys
  • a box

Safety

No ribbons, elastic or laces: only socks, hats and scarves.

Don’t tie fabric around the neck of the toy or your child.

Silent ninja trail

5-6 yearsmovementno mess20 min

Your child tiptoes along a trail of cushions and books to grab a secret message while you sleep as a dragon holding a bell.

Setup

Lay a winding trail of cushions, books and soft toys on the rug. Write a short message and put it at the end of the trail.

Steps

  1. Sit at the end of the trail with your eyes closed and a bell in your hand: you are the sleeping dragon.
  2. Let your child tiptoe along the trail, grab the message and sneak back quietly.
  3. If you hear a rustle, ring the bell: the ninja starts the trail again from the beginning.
  4. When the ninja makes it back with the message, unfold the note and read it out loud.
  5. Swap roles: your child becomes the dragon, and you tiptoe along the trail.

What you need

  • cushions
  • books
  • soft toys
  • a sheet of paper
  • a pen
  • a bell or rattle

Safety

Go around or step over the books and cushions rather than on them, so no one slips.

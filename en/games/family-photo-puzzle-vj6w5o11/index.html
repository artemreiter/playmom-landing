Family photo puzzle

Fit together large photos of family members cut in half, and name who’s whole again.

Steps

What you need

  • 2 photos of family members printed on paper
  • cardboard from a box
  • a glue stick
  • scissors

Safety

Cut each photo in half only, no small pieces, and make sure the cardboard has no staples or sharp corners.

If your baby chews on a piece or it gets soggy, take it away.

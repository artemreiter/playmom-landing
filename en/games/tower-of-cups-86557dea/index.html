Tower of cups

12-15 monthsfine motor skillsno prepno mess7 min

Build a little tower from cups, let your child knock it down, then let them stack the cups and decide when it's time to tumble.

Setup

Set 4 sturdy plastic cups on the floor.

Steps

  1. Stack one cup on another: “A tower.” Add a third.
  2. Invite your child to knock the tower down: “Crash.” Enjoy together how the cups roll.
  3. Give your child a cup and help them place it on top, even if it falls right away.
  4. Ask: “Higher, or crash?” Let your child decide, and have them start the next tower.

What you need

  • 4 sturdy plastic cups

Safety

Use sturdy cups that don't crack, and remove a cracked one right away.

Stay close while your child plays.

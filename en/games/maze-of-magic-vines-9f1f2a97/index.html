Maze of magic vines

3-4 yearsgross motor skillsno mess25 min

Tape vines onto the floor and hang paper vines from the table, then let your child make their way to a hidden artefact without touching them.

Setup

Tape 3 zigzags onto the floor, and tape paper strips to the edge of a sturdy table down to the floor. Hide a toy artefact under the table.

Steps

  1. Say that the vines guard the artefact and wake up if touched.
  2. Your child steps over the zigzags without stepping on them, while you whisper: “Quiet, the vines are sleeping.”
  3. Under the table, your child crawls through the paper vines on all fours or on their belly, whichever they choose.
  4. Swap roles: your child hides the artefact and resets the zigzags, while you make your way through the maze.

What you need

  • masking tape
  • paper cut into long strips
  • a sturdy table
  • a toy

Safety

Don’t string up rope, ribbon or scarves: use only tape and tearable paper for the vines.

Keep your child off the table and chairs.

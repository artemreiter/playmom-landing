Kitchen mood orchestra

15-18 monthsemotionsno mess19 min

Bang on a pot, a lid and a container with your child, loud when the teddy bear is happy and quiet when it sleeps.

Steps

  1. Tap each one: “Boom” on the pot, “Clang” on the lid, “Tock” on the container. Give your child a spoon.

What you need

  • a pot
  • a metal lid
  • a plastic container
  • 2 wooden spoons
  • a soft toy

Safety

Use only the pot, lid, container and spoons, no grater, glasses or grains.

Keep loud banging brief and stop if your child flinches.

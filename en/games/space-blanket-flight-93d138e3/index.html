Space blanket flight

3-4 yearsmovementno mess16 min

Your child sits on a blanket while you slowly pull them around the room from one cushion planet to the next, whichever they choose.

Setup

Spread a blanket on a smooth floor well away from furniture, and scatter cushion planets around the room.

Steps

  1. Let your child sit in the middle of the blanket and hold onto the edges with both hands.
  2. Pull the blanket slowly and narrate: “There’s a red planet ahead, hold on tight.”
  3. Your child points to which planet to fly to next, and makes up who lives there.
  4. Swap roles: your child pulls a soft toy along on the blanket, and you name the planets.

What you need

  • a big blanket
  • cushions
  • a soft toy

Safety

Keep your child seated the whole time, and pull slowly, with no jerks or swinging.

Steer clear of furniture corners and look where you’re going.

Fairy-tale box tunnel

21-24 monthsgross motor skillsno mess22 min

Join two boxes into a tunnel on the floor, and your baby crawls over a cushion mountain into the cave to a hidden toy.

Setup

Lay two large boxes with no staples end to end on the floor, open at both ends, and join them with masking tape.

Steps

  1. Hide a toy at the tunnel exit: “Someone’s waiting in the cave.”
  2. Put a sofa cushion at the entrance: your baby climbs over the mountain and dives into the cave.
  3. Peek into the tunnel from the other end and call: “Peekaboo, crawl to me.”
  4. Let your baby hide the toy at one of the exits themselves, and you guess where it is.

What you need

  • 2 large cardboard boxes
  • masking tape
  • a sofa cushion
  • a favourite toy

Safety

The boxes lie on the floor end to end, not on top of each other.

Don’t put cushions or blankets inside, and don’t block the exits while your baby is in the tunnel.

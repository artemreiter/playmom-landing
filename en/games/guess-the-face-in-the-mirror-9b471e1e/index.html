Guess the face in the mirror

4-5 yearsemotions18 min

Make a happy, sad or surprised face in the mirror, and your child guesses the feeling and draws it.

Setup

Stand by the hallway mirror or put a shatterproof mirror on the table. Put a whiteboard marker and a tissue nearby.

Steps

  1. Make a happy, sad or surprised face in the mirror. Let your child guess the feeling.
  2. Let your child draw that feeling on the mirror: a circle, eyebrows and a mouth.
  3. Stand so the drawing sits over your face in the reflection, and make that face again.
  4. Swap: your child makes a face, you guess and draw. Wipe off the drawing and keep playing.

What you need

  • a mirror
  • a whiteboard marker
  • a tissue

Safety

Draw only with a whiteboard marker, and only on a shatterproof or wall-mounted mirror.

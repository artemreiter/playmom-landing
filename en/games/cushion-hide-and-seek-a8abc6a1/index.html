Cushion hide and seek

12-15 monthsgross motor skillsno mess13 min

Hide toys behind cushions laid out on the floor, and your baby moves them aside, finds the toys and hides them too.

Steps

  1. Point to the bit sticking out: “Who’s hiding there?” Move the cushion: “Teddy.”

What you need

  • 3 sofa cushions
  • 3 soft toys

Safety

Don’t stack the cushions into a tower or a wall, so nothing can fall on your baby.

Stay close and make sure your baby’s face doesn’t stay pressed into a cushion. Toys with no small parts.

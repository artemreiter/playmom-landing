Mood dance

21-24 monthsemotionsno mess11 min

Dance to a fast song and a slow one, and let your child choose which one to dance to next.

Setup

Find one fast, happy song and one slow, calm song on your phone.

Steps

  1. Put on the fast song and dance together like bunnies: clap and stamp.
  2. Put on the slow one and move gently like a cloud: sway your arms and turn slowly.
  3. Ask: “What kind of music is it, happy or calm?” Suggest the word if your child doesn’t answer.
  4. Let your child choose the next song: “Bunny or cloud?” Copy their dance.

What you need

  • a phone or speaker with music

Safety

Dance in a clear space, with no slippery rugs or sharp corners nearby.

Keep the volume low.

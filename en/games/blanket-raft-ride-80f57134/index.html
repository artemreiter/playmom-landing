Blanket raft ride

24-30 monthsgross motor skillsno mess10 min

Your baby sits on a blanket raft while you slowly pull them across the floor between cushion reefs, rocking gently like waves.

Setup

Spread a blanket on the floor well away from furniture, and set three cushions around it as reefs.

Steps

  1. Your baby sits in the middle of the raft and holds the edge with both hands.
  2. Pull the blanket slowly and rock it gently: “Waves coming, hold on.”
  3. Sail around the reefs and ask your baby which one to head for next.
  4. Stop by a reef and let your baby paddle with their hands like oars.
  5. Swap roles: your baby pulls a teddy bear along on a towel, while you make waves with your hands.

What you need

  • a big blanket
  • three cushions
  • a small towel
  • a soft teddy bear

Safety

Your baby sits rather than stands, and you pull slowly, with no jerks or sharp turns.

Keep clear of furniture corners, and keep the cushions on the floor only.

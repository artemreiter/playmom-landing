Rustling orchestra

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno mess11 min

Rustle whole sheets of paper and a scarf, and your baby scrunches them in their hands and chooses what to rustle next.

Setup

Sit with your baby on a blanket. Put a whole sheet of baking paper, a sheet of wrapping paper and a scarf beside you.

Steps

  1. Scrunch the baking paper where your baby can see: “Shhh.” Wait to see if they reach for it.
  2. Give your baby the sheet and help them squeeze it in their hands. Copy the rustle with your voice.
  3. Rub the wrapping paper: “Crunch, crunch.” Then stroke your baby’s palm with the scarf.
  4. Put the sheet and the scarf in front of your baby. Play with whichever one they pick.

What you need

  • baking paper
  • a sheet of wrapping paper
  • a thin scarf
  • a blanket

Safety

Whole, large sheets only: no foil, cling film or bags.

If your baby tears off bits or mouths the paper, take it away and pick up the scraps.

Don’t drape the scarf over your baby’s face or head.

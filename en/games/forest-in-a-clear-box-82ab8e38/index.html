Forest in a clear box

24-30 monthsfine motor skillsno mess14 min

Your child moves socks, a scarf and a toy into a clear container with tongs and decides which becomes a bush or a bear.

Steps

  1. Squeeze the tongs, pick up a sock ball and drop it in the container: “This is a bush.”

What you need

  • a large clear plastic container
  • plastic salad tongs
  • 2-3 pairs of socks
  • a scarf
  • a soft toy

Safety

Everything is bigger than your child’s fist: no cotton wool balls, pompoms, fabric scraps or dry leaves.

Plastic tongs with no sharp edges.

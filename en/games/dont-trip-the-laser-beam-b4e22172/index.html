Don’t trip the laser beam

24-30 monthsgross motor skillsno mess18 min

Step over tape beams on the floor and crawl under an adult’s arm without setting off the alarm.

Steps

  1. Step over the beams one by one, and if anyone touches a strip, beep like an alarm: “Beep beep beep.”

What you need

  • masking tape
  • a soft toy

Safety

The only beams are tape on the floor and your arm: no string, ribbon or yarn stretched across the walkway.

Play on a rug or non-slip floor, there’s no need to jump over the beams.

Explorer’s corridor

6-9 monthsgross motor skillsno mess9 min

Build a corridor of cushions on the floor and call your baby from the far end to crawl or reach for a toy.

Steps

  1. Show your baby 2 toys, then put the one they reach for at the far end of the corridor.

3 more steps in the PlayMom app

2 games free, then by subscription with a trial period

Open in the app

What you need

  • 4 sofa cushions
  • 2 bright toys

Safety

Keep the cushions flat, with no throw or chairs on top, so nothing can fall on your baby or cover their face.

The toys are bigger than your baby’s fist and have no small parts.

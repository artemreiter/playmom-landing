Play dough bakery

30-36 monthsfine motor skills25 min

Shape pastries from play dough together, decorate them with pieces of a different colour, and sell them to toy customers from a display counter.

Setup

Put play dough in three colours on a board, with a tray as the display counter and two toys nearby.

Steps

  1. Announce the bakery is open, and show how to roll a truffle and flatten a cookie.
  2. Let your child choose what to bake first and shape the base themselves.
  3. Decorate together: pinch off dough in a different colour for cherries, and press little dents in with a finger.
  4. Set the treats out on the display counter: the toys buy them, and your child names each pastry.

What you need

  • play dough in three colours
  • a dough board
  • a tray
  • two toys

Safety

No grains, beads or pasta are needed: decorations are made only from dough.

Use play dough with a suitable age label, and it’s not for eating: if your child puts it in their mouth, end the game.

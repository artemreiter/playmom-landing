Colourful sock balls

18-21 monthssensesno mess22 min

Pour sock balls of 2 colours from hand to hand together, and sort them into bowls, naming each colour.

Steps

  1. Pour the sock balls from the container into a bowl and back, and let your baby copy you with their hands.

What you need

  • 6 pairs of socks in 2 colours
  • a container
  • 2 bowls

Safety

Don’t use dry pasta or food colouring: the game uses only sock balls bigger than your baby’s fist.

Use socks with no stickers or pompoms, and re-roll any ball that comes undone.

Mood boxes

24-30 monthsemotionsno mess27 min

Sort soft items together into three boxes with a happy, sad and angry face, then comfort a teddy bear who feels sad.

Steps

  1. Show the happy box: “Happy, I want to play.” Let your baby choose what to put inside.

Open in the app

What you need

  • three shoeboxes
  • a marker
  • a ball
  • a soft scarf
  • a plush teddy bear
  • a book
  • a small cushion

Safety

All the items are bigger than your baby’s fist, with no plastic film, clay, pencils or bubbles.

Never drape the scarf over your baby’s face or head.

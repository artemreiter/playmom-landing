Lullaby for the doll

18-21 monthsemotionsno prepno messbedtime9 min

Rock a doll together with your child, tuck it in under a small cloth and let your child pick who to put to bed next.

Setup

Sit a doll or soft toy next to your child and set out a small towel to use as a blanket.

Steps

  1. Say: “The doll is tired.” Rock it in your arms and sing a quiet lullaby.
  2. Give the doll to your child: let them rock it themselves while you hum along.
  3. Let your child lay the doll down and cover it with the towel. Whisper: “Shh, the doll is asleep.”
  4. Ask who to put to bed next: the doll again or another toy. Let your child choose.

What you need

  • a doll or soft toy
  • a small towel

Safety

Cover only the doll with the towel, never your child's face or head.

Use a doll without small parts that could come off.

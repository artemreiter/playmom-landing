Talking into a saucepan

6-9 monthsspeechno prepno messbedtime7 min

Say long syllables into a saucepan so your voice hums, your baby answers with babble, and you copy their sound.

Setup

Sit on the floor facing your baby, with a big saucepan on its side between you and a smaller one next to it.

Steps

  1. Lean in and say a long “ba-a-a” into the pan: your voice will hum.
  2. Say “da-da” into it and wait, looking at your baby.
  3. Turn the pan towards your baby and wait for their sound: it will hum too.
  4. Repeat your baby’s sound into the pan like an echo, and wait for an answer again.
  5. Slide the small pan over: whichever one your baby reaches for, talk into that one.

What you need

  • a large saucepan
  • a smaller saucepan

Safety

The pans are cold, with no lids and nothing small inside.

Speak softly: the pan makes the sound louder.

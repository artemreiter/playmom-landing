Whose shadow is this

15-18 monthsthinkingno mess11 min

Show your toddler the shadow of a familiar toy on the wall, and let them recognise it, choose the next one and watch the shadow grow.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch on the wall and wave your hand in the beam: “Look, the shadow’s waving.”

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a light-coloured wall
  • a ball
  • a soft toy
  • a plastic cup

Safety

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover, and never shine it in anyone’s eyes.

Use only toys bigger than your toddler’s fist; if the half-light feels unsettling, open the curtains.

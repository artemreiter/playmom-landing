Sock snowfall

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno mess11 min

Drop soft rolled-up socks around your baby, and they collect the snowballs in a chest, then make it snow on you.

Setup

Roll 8-10 pairs of socks into soft balls. Put a basket or box on the floor: this is the chest.

Steps

  1. Stand next to your baby and drop the balls on the floor one at a time: “It’s snowing.”
  2. Let your baby pick up a snowball and carry it to the chest. Cheer each one: “One more.”
  3. When the snow runs out, invite your baby to throw snowballs into the chest from a couple of steps away.
  4. Swap: your baby makes it snow and you collect the snowballs in the chest.

What you need

  • 8-10 pairs of socks
  • a basket or box

Safety

Drop the balls next to your baby, not on their face or head.

The box has no staples or sharp edges, and there are no cables on the floor.

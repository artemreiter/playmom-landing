Hide behind the armchair

15-18 monthsemotionsno prepno mess7 min

Hide behind an armchair with your feet showing, wait for your baby to find you, then swap.

Setup

No preparation needed. Choose a hiding place in the same room, such as an armchair or sofa.

Steps

  1. Say “I’m hiding” and, while your baby watches, hide behind the armchair with your feet in view.
  2. Call softly: “Where am I?” Wait until your baby comes over or looks your way.
  3. When your baby finds you, peek out with a smile: “Peekaboo, here I am.”
  4. Swap: your baby hides, and you wonder out loud where they are and find them.

Safety

Hide in the same room, not behind a door or near a window with curtain or blind cords.

If your baby gets scared or cries, peek out straight away and give them a cuddle.

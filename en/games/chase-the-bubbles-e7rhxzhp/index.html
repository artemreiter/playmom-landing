Chase the bubbles

18-21 monthsgross motor skills11 min

Blow bubbles in different directions while your child chases them and pops them with their palms and a finger.

Steps

  1. Blow bubbles in front of your child and show how to clap a bubble between your palms: “Pop.”

What you need

  • a tub of bubbles with a wand

Safety

Keep the mixture and the wand with you: your child doesn’t blow into it or drink the mixture. Don’t blow bubbles into your child’s face.

Indoors, play on a rug; outdoors, on flat ground: the floor under bubbles gets slippery.

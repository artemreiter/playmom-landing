A tunnel with a firefly

24-30 monthsmovementno mess17 min

Drape a blanket over the table to make a tunnel, and let your toddler crawl after the torch beam, catch the firefly and find a soft toy at the end.

Steps

  1. Sit a toy at the far end, sit at the near end yourself, and shine the torch into the tunnel: “Look, a firefly.”

What you need

  • a dining table
  • a blanket
  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a soft toy

Safety

Use only a sturdy table with no chairs, and keep both ends open; remove the blanket if your toddler pulls it down.

Only you hold the torch with the screwed-down battery cover, and never shine it in anyone’s eyes.

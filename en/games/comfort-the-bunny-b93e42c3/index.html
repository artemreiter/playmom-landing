Comfort the bunny

18-30 monthsemotionsno mess13 min

The bunny takes a tumble, and your child chooses how to make it better: a napkin on the bump, a blanket or a stroke.

Steps

  1. Gently drop the bunny: “Oh, it fell.” Hug it and stroke it: “Poor bunny.”

What you need

Safety

Don’t warm the napkin: no hot water bottles or heat packs.

Only cover the toy with the blanket, never your child’s face.

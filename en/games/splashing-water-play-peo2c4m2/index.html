Splashing water play

9-12 monthssenses10 min

Splash your palms with your baby in warm water on a tray, catch a floating toy, and take turns making a splash.

Setup

Spread a towel on the floor, set a wide tray on it, and pour in warm water about 1 to 2 cm deep.

Steps

  1. Sit your baby in front of the tray and slap your palm on the water: “Splash.”
  2. Let your baby slap the water and trail their fingers through it, while you narrate: “Warm, wet, splash.”
  3. Drop a floating toy into the water and push a little wave toward it with your hand: let your baby catch it.
  4. Take turns splashing: wait for your baby to slap the water, then copy them.

What you need

  • a wide shallow tray
  • warm water
  • a towel
  • a floating toy

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach, keep the water warm and no deeper than the bottom of the tray, and pour it out right after playing.

The toy is bigger than your baby’s fist and has no small parts.

Prints in soft dough

12-15 monthssenses22 min

Knead homemade salt-free dough together and press dimples into it with your fingers and circles with the rim of a cup.

Setup

Mix a salt-free dough from a cup of flour, half a cup of water and a spoonful of oil. Cover the table with a wipeable cloth.

Steps

  1. Put a palm-sized lump of dough in front of your baby and squeeze your own lump: “Squish, squish.”
  2. Let your baby squeeze, pat and pull the dough however they like, and copy what they do.
  3. Press a finger into the dough, show the dimple, then press the rim of a cup into it: “Circle.”
  4. Give your baby the cup: let them decide where to press a circle, and you add a dimple beside it.

What you need

  • flour
  • water
  • vegetable oil
  • a plastic cup
  • a wipeable cloth

Safety

Salty dough is dangerous if your baby eats it, so leave out the salt.

Raw dough isn’t food: if your baby pulls it towards their mouth, take the piece away.

If your baby is allergic to wheat, use rice flour instead.

Compliment circle

4-5 yearsemotionsno prepno messbedtime11 min

Sit as a family in a circle and pass a soft ball around: whoever holds it pays the person next to them a specific compliment.

Steps

  1. Start yourself: pass the ball to your child and say something specific: “I love how you build towers.”

What you need

Safety

Playing doctor

18-21 monthsemotionsno prepno messbedtime11 min

Treat a sick teddy bear together: take his temperature with a spoon, give pretend medicine, and wrap a scarf bandage around his paw.

Steps

  1. Say in a sad voice: “Teddy isn’t feeling well, he needs some treatment.”

What you need

  • a soft toy
  • a child’s spoon
  • a scarf

Safety

Don’t use sticks, stickers or real medicine: the treatment is make-believe, and the spoon stays empty.

Don’t tie the scarf on or drape it over your baby’s face.

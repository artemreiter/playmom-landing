Cushion corridor crawl

9-12 monthsgross motor skillsno mess12 min

Build a corridor of cushions on the floor, peek in from the other end, and call your baby to crawl through to you.

Steps

  1. Sit your baby at the entrance, peek into the corridor from the other end and say: “Peekaboo, crawl to me.”

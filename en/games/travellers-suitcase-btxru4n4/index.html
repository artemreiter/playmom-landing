Traveller’s suitcase

4-6 yearsindependenceno mess22 min

Your child picks a destination, packs a backpack from a picture list and explains at customs what each item is for.

Setup

Take a backpack, a sheet of paper and a pencil. Let your child choose where to go: the North Pole, the jungle or the Moon.

Steps

  1. Ask what will be useful there. Draw a picture list of 5 things and add one silly one, such as a swim ring.
  2. Your child gathers the things from around the house into the backpack and ticks off each picture.
  3. Set up customs: your child lays out the things, explains what each one is for and finds the odd one out.
  4. Swap: your child chooses a new place, and you pack the backpack and put in something wrong on purpose.

What you need

  • a backpack or bag
  • a sheet of paper
  • a pencil
  • things from around the house

Safety

Your child only takes things from within reach, without climbing on furniture. Medicines, knives and batteries are never on the list.

Mushrooms in the meadow

21-24 monthsfine motor skillsno messbedtime15 min

Grow mushrooms together on a towel meadow, using tubes for stalks and cups for caps, then let your little one plant a whole forest.

Setup

Spread out a green towel as the meadow, and put 4 toilet paper tubes, 4 plastic cups and a basket nearby.

Steps

  1. Stand a tube on the meadow, cover it with an upside-down cup, and say: “A mushroom grew.”
  2. Give your little one a tube and a cup, and let them set the stalk and fit the cap themselves.
  3. Ask where the next mushroom will grow, and name the colour of its cap.
  4. Swap roles: your little one plants mushrooms wherever they like, while you gather them into the basket.

What you need

  • 4 toilet paper tubes
  • 4 plastic cups
  • a green towel
  • a basket

Safety

Use tubes and cups bigger than your little one’s fist, with no chenille wire or sponge needed.

If your little one chews a tube and it gets soggy, take it away.

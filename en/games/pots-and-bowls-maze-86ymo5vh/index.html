Pots and bowls maze

18-30 monthsthinkingno mess22 min

Build a floor maze from a pot, bowls and a board, and roll a ball through the tunnel, gate and ramp.

Setup

Lay a path on the floor: a lightweight pot on its side, 2 upturned plastic bowls with a gap between them, and a chopping board resting with one edge on a book.

Steps

  1. Push the ball into the pot and catch it coming out the other side: “Where’s the ball? There it is.”
  2. Let your baby roll the ball through the gate and down the ramp, while you catch it at the end.
  3. Give them a second ball of a different size and ask whether it will fit through the gate.
  4. Let your baby rearrange the bowls and pot their own way, and roll the ball through the new maze.

What you need

  • a lightweight pot
  • 2 plastic bowls
  • a chopping board
  • a book
  • 2 balls of different sizes

Safety

Both balls should be bigger than your baby’s fist: skip ping-pong balls and other small ones.

Use only lightweight, unbreakable dishes, no glass, and don’t let your baby stand on the board or pot.

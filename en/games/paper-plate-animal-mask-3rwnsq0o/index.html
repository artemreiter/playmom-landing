Paper plate animal mask

24-30 monthsspeechno mess23 min

Draw an animal face on a paper plate, take turns hiding behind it and talk in animal voices.

Setup

Put two paper plates and chunky wax crayons on the table.

Steps

  1. Let your child choose an animal: a cat, a dog or an owl. Draw big eyes and a nose on a plate.
  2. Let your child add whiskers or spots. Any scribbles will do.
  3. Hold the plate up to your face, peek out from the side and say in an animal voice: “Meow, who’s there?”
  4. Give the mask to your child: let them hide and answer in an animal voice while you guess who it is.
  5. Draw a second animal and chat: how each one talks and what it likes to eat.

What you need

  • two paper plates
  • chunky wax crayons

Safety

The mask is held in the hand, with no elastic or ties.

Make sure the crayons don’t end up in your child’s mouth.

Hand shadow theatre

21-24 monthsthinkingno mess27 min

Shine a torch on toys and hands, and your baby touches the shadows on the wall and chooses whose shadow to show next.

Setup

Draw the curtains, leaving the room dim, and sit two or three toys by a light-coloured wall.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch on a toy and let your baby touch its shadow on the wall.
  2. Make a dog with your hand in the beam and wiggle your fingers: “Woof woof.”
  3. Help your baby hold their hand up to the light and find its shadow on the wall.
  4. Move a toy closer to the torch and the shadow grows; move it away and it shrinks.
  5. Let your baby choose whose shadow to show next, and you shine the torch.

What you need

  • a torch
  • 2-3 toys

Safety

Keep the torch, with its battery cover screwed down, in your own hands at all times.

Don’t shine it in your baby’s eyes.

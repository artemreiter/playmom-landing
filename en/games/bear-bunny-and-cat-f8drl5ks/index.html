Bear, bunny and cat

15-18 monthsmovementno prepno mess7 min

Show the walk of a bear, a bunny and a cat in turn, then copy whichever animal your baby picks.

Setup

Nothing to get ready. Clear a little space on the rug.

Steps

  1. Say: “Here comes the bear,” and waddle along, rocking from foot to foot.
  2. Put your hands up by your head as ears and bounce on the spot: “The bunny hops.”
  3. Get down on all fours and meow: “The cat creeps along,” and let your baby crawl alongside you.
  4. Ask: “Who’s next, the bear or the cat?” and copy whatever your baby shows you.

Safety

Your baby doesn’t need to jump, a little bounce in the knees is enough for the bunny.

Play on the rug, well away from furniture corners and steps.

Water rescuer

12-15 monthsattention17 min

Big bath toys float in a basin of warm water, and your child rescues them with a small sieve and carries them to shore.

Steps

  1. “The animals are in the water. Who will save them?” Scoop one toy out with the sieve and set it on the shore.

What you need

  • a basin
  • warm water
  • 3 bath toys bigger than your child's fist
  • a plastic sieve
  • a towelling cloth
  • a bowl
  • a big towel

Safety

Stay within arm's reach, the water is shallow; check the temperature with your hand.

Pour the water out right after play.

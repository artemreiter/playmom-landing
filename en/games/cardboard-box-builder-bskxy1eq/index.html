Cardboard box builder

30-36 monthsthinking35 min

Turn a large box into a car or a rocket: your child chooses which one, draws windows and buttons, and sets off.

Steps

  1. Let your child climb inside and choose: “What will this be, a car or a rocket?”

What you need

  • a large cardboard box
  • 2 small boxes
  • a paper plate
  • wide tape
  • markers
  • scissors

Safety

Never stack the box on another one or cover it with anything while your child is inside.

Keep the scissors to yourself; no stickers needed.

Magic appearing pictures

15-18 monthsemotions22 min

Paint over sheets with invisible drawings in watercolour, and your baby picks a sheet and a colour and draws their own secret.

Setup

Beforehand, draw a circle, a star and a wave in white wax crayon on three sheets. Mix two watercolour paints and lay down a wipe-clean tablecloth.

Steps

  1. Show two white sheets: let your baby point to where the secret is hidden.
  2. Paint over that sheet with a wide brush while your baby watches: “Oh, a circle.”
  3. Paint the next sheet in the colour your baby chooses.
  4. Give your baby the crayon and a clean sheet: let them scribble their own secret, and you reveal it.

What you need

  • a white wax crayon
  • 4 sheets of paper
  • watercolour paints
  • a wide brush
  • a wipe-clean tablecloth

Safety

You keep the paint and brush at all times; your baby only gets the chunky crayon.

If your baby puts the crayon or wet paper in their mouth, take them away.

Where’s that rattle

9-12 monthsattentionno prepno mess6 min

Hide a rattle under a cushion while your baby watches, shake it and wait to see if they turn towards the sound.

Setup

No preparation needed. Put a rattle and a cushion or folded blanket nearby.

Steps

  1. Shake the rattle in front of your baby, then hide it under the cushion while they watch.
  2. Slide your hand under the cushion and shake it: “Can you hear it? Where’s that sound?” Wait to see where your baby turns.
  3. If your baby reaches for the cushion, let them lift it off. If not, lift the edge yourself: “Here it is.”
  4. Let your baby shake the rattle, then hide it somewhere else: behind your back or under the edge of the blanket.

What you need

  • a rattle
  • a cushion or blanket

Safety

The rattle is in one piece, right for your baby’s age, with no parts coming loose.

Cover only the toy with the cushion or blanket, never your baby.

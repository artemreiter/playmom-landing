Who lives in the little house

18-21 monthsthinkingno mess30 min

Your toddler lifts the lids off box houses, finds the toys hiding inside, remembers who lives where and hides the toys for you.

Steps

  1. Knock on a box: “Knock knock, who lives here?” Lift the lid and say hello to the toy.

What you need

  • three shoeboxes with lids
  • a ball bigger than your toddler’s fist
  • a soft toy
  • a large toy car

Safety

Use boxes with no staples, tape or sharp edges, and take a box away if your toddler puts the cardboard in their mouth.

Treasure in a soft ocean

21-24 monthsthinkingno mess17 min

Your child digs through a basket of towels and socks with their hands, finds hidden things and hides them for you.

Setup

Fill a basket or washing-up bowl with scrunched-up towels, socks and scarves. Hide a ball, a block and a wooden spoon inside.

Steps

  1. Put your hands in the basket: “What a soft ocean. There’s treasure hidden here.”
  2. Let your child dig with their hands. If they feel something hard, ask: “What’s that?” and wait while they pull it out.
  3. Name the find and say one word about it: “Ball. Round.” “Spoon. Smooth.”
  4. Let your child decide: hide the treasure again or swap, so they hide and you search.

What you need

  • a basket or washing-up bowl
  • towels
  • socks
  • scarves
  • a ball
  • a block
  • a wooden spoon

Safety

Don’t use cotton wool, yarn, pompoms or beads: only fabric and things bigger than your child’s fist.

Don’t drape fabric over your child’s head, and don’t let them climb into the basket.

Put a sock on your hand, talk for it in a funny voice and give your baby a sock of their own so they can chat.

Steps

  1. Open and close the sock’s mouth and say in a funny voice: “Hello, I’m Buzzy.” Wait for any reply: a word, a sound or a gesture.

Catch the light spot

12-15 monthsattentionno mess10 min

Move a spot of torchlight across the wall and floor, and your baby chases it and pats it with their hand.

Setup

Draw the curtains, leaving the room dim, and sit your baby facing a light-coloured wall.

Steps

  1. Shine the torch on the wall next to your baby and slowly move the spot: “Oops, it ran away.”
  2. Bring the spot down to your baby’s hands, and when a hand covers it, say: “Got it.”
  3. Switch the torch off for a second and back on somewhere else: “There it is.”
  4. Move the spot across the floor so your baby can stamp on it or crawl after it.
  5. Let your baby point to where the spot should go, and move it there.

What you need

  • a torch

Safety

Keep the torch with the screwed-down cover in your hands at all times, and don’t shine it in your baby’s eyes.

Clear the floor of anything someone could trip over.

Paper rain

12-15 monthssenses13 min

Tear paper into big pieces together, toss them up like rain and collect them in a box.

Setup

Take four sheets of coloured or white paper and a box. Sit on the floor with your baby.

Steps

  1. Slowly tear a sheet in half: “Rrrip.” Give your baby the second sheet and help them tear the edge.
  2. Tear each half in half once more and no smaller, so the pieces stay big.
  3. Toss the pieces up: “It’s raining.” Pick them up off the floor straight away with your baby.
  4. Ask what’s next: more rain or putting the paper in the box. Pick up the pieces straight away each time.

What you need

  • four sheets of coloured or white paper
  • a box

Safety

Keep the pieces bigger than your baby’s palm and pick them up off the floor straight away. If your baby puts paper in their mouth, take it away and end the game.

Use plain paper, no foil, magazines or toilet paper.

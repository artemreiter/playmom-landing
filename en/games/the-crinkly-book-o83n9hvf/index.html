The crinkly book

9-12 monthsindependenceno mess7 min

Crinkle a soft book together with your baby, then let them turn and scrunch the pages while you sit beside them.

Setup

Sit on the floor next to your baby and put a soft crinkle book in front of them.

Steps

  1. Scrunch a page and say: “Crinkle, crinkle.” Wait for your baby to reach for it.
  2. Let your baby turn and scrunch the pages however they like. Name the sound and the picture on the open page.
  3. When your baby holds the book out to you, crinkle it yourself and give it back.
  4. If they lose interest, open a new page or put the book away until next time.

What you need

  • a soft crinkle book

Safety

Before playing, check that the seams are intact and nothing is coming loose. Stay close while your baby plays.

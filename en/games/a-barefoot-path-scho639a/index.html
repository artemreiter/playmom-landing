A barefoot path

24-30 monthssenses20 min

Lay out a path of towels, a blanket and newspaper on the rug, and let your toddler walk it barefoot and tell you how it feels underfoot.

Setup

Lay a towel, a folded blanket, newspaper and a wrung-out damp towel side by side on the rug.

Steps

  1. Take your own and your toddler’s socks off, and walk the path first: “Ooh, this one’s soft.”
  2. Lead your toddler along the path by the hand and ask: “Soft or wet? Does it crinkle?”
  3. Repeat any word or sound your toddler makes, and offer one of your own.
  4. Let your toddler choose what to add to the path, such as a mat or a t-shirt, and lead you across it.

What you need

  • a towel
  • a blanket
  • newspaper
  • a damp towel
  • a rug

Safety

Keep the path on the rug only, and hold your toddler’s hand the whole time; afterwards, remove the damp towel and dry their feet.

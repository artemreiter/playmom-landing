Jungle obstacle route

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno mess22 min

Work through a small obstacle course together: step over a log, cross the stepping stones, and crawl through a cave made from your legs.

Steps

  1. Go through the route first yourself, saying: “Step over the log, walk on the stones.”

What you need

  • a sofa cushion
  • masking tape
  • a small bucket
  • 3 soft toys

Safety

No ropes or chairs needed: your baby only crawls under your legs.

Keep the route on the rug, with toys that are soft and bigger than your baby’s fist.

Stand steady, and don’t squeeze your child between your legs.

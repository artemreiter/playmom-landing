Frozen treasures

21-24 monthssenses25 min

Freeze large toys in ice, and your baby pours warm water over it to set the treasures free.

Steps

  1. Tip the ice into a washing-up bowl and ask: “There’s treasure inside, how do we get it out?”

What you need

  • a plastic container
  • 2 large toys
  • a washing-up bowl
  • a jug of warm water
  • a small cup
  • a towel

Safety

Water as warm as for washing hands, and only a little in the bottom of the bowl; stay within arm’s reach.

Only large toys in the ice: no buttons, coins, beads, salt or food colouring.

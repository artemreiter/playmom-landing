Torch in the cave

15-18 monthsattentionno mess17 min

Shine a torch under a table covered with a blanket and let your baby crawl to the toy in the beam.

Steps

  1. Sit at the entrance, switch on the torch and shine it on one toy: “Look, who’s there?”

What you need

  • a dining table
  • a blanket
  • a torch
  • three large soft toys

Safety

Keep the torch, with its battery cover firmly closed, in your own hands at all times, and shine it at the toys, not into eyes.

Use only a sturdy table and keep the entrance open.

Jumping on clouds

2-3 yearsmovementno mess16 min

Lay cushions on the rug and move from cloud to cloud without touching the floor, while your child picks the way.

Steps

  1. Say: “The cushions are clouds, the floor is the ground.” Cross the clouds first yourself.

Foil costume studio

4-5 yearsfine motor skillsno mess20 min

Wrap small figures in aluminium foil, lift off the finished costumes as hollow sculptures, and come up with names for them.

Setup

Cut aluminium foil into squares of 10 to 20 cm, put the scissors away, and set five figures on a tray.

Steps

  1. Show how to wrap a figure in foil and press it in around the ears and paws.
  2. Let your child choose a figure and make its costume themselves: crumpling, smoothing and tucking in the edges.
  3. Carefully lift the foil off: the shape stays behind, and your child names the sculpture.
  4. Swap roles: your child orders up a hat or boots, and you make the costume.

What you need

  • aluminium foil
  • five small figures
  • a tray
  • scissors for the adult

Safety

Foil and figures don’t go in the mouth; if there are children under three nearby, gather up any scraps right away.

What’s in the bag

24-30 monthssensesno messbedtime14 min

Your toddler feels for a familiar object inside a pillowcase, says whether it’s soft or hard, and guesses what it is.

Setup

Put a wooden spoon, a soft toy, a ball, a plastic cup and a mitten inside a pillowcase.

Steps

  1. Feel an object through the pillowcase and say: “There’s something soft in here, is it the teddy?”
  2. Let your toddler reach in and take out one object without peeking inside.
  3. Ask: “Soft or hard? Smooth or bumpy?” and offer a word if your toddler stays quiet.
  4. Let your toddler guess, or simply pull the object out and check, then name it together.
  5. Swap roles: your toddler hides an object in the pillowcase, and you guess.

What you need

  • a pillowcase
  • a wooden spoon
  • a soft toy
  • a ball bigger than your toddler’s fist
  • a plastic cup
  • a mitten

Safety

Hold the pillowcase yourself and never put it over your toddler’s head; use only objects bigger than their fist.

Toys in jelly

15-18 monthssenses22 min

Touch cool, colourful jelly together and pull out the big toys hidden inside it.

Steps

  1. Touch the jelly yourself first and give the container a shake: “It wobbles.”

What you need

  • gelatine
  • food colouring
  • a shallow plastic container
  • three large bath toys
  • a wipe-clean tablecloth
  • a towel

Safety

The toys are bigger than your child’s fist, with no small parts. Make the jelly only from gelatine, water and food colouring.

Stay close and wash your child’s hands after playing.

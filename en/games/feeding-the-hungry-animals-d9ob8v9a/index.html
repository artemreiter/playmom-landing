Feeding the hungry animals

18-21 monthsemotionsno mess40 min

Sit toys on boxes with mouth slots, and your baby chooses who to feed and pushes the treat inside.

Setup

Cut a mouth slot in the lids of two shoeboxes, sit a soft toy on each, and cut 4 treats bigger than your palm from cardboard.

Steps

  1. Speak for the kitty, “Miaow, I’m hungry,” and drop a treat into the slot.
  2. Give your baby the treats: let them choose who to feed and push the treat into the mouth.
  3. After each feed, answer for the animal: “Yum, delicious.”
  4. Swap: your baby shows you who’s hungry, and you do the feeding.

What you need

  • 2 shoeboxes
  • 2 soft toys
  • cardboard
  • scissors

Safety

Cut the slots beforehand without your baby around, and put the knife and scissors away straight after.

Boxes with no staples; treats bigger than your baby’s palm.

Little animal shadow theater

15-18 monthsattentionno messbedtime32 min

Shine a flashlight on the wall to show soft toy shadows, while your child guesses the animal and then moves it in the beam.

Steps

  1. Shine the light on a light wall and bring the bunny into the beam: a big shadow appears. “Who is it?”

What you need

  • a flashlight with a screwed battery cover
  • 3 soft animal toys
  • a light wall

Safety

Keep the flashlight with a screwed battery cover in your hand at all times, pointed at the wall, never at eyes.

Turn the light on if your child feels unsure.

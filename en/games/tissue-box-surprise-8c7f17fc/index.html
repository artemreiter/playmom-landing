Tissue box surprise

6-9 monthsfine motor skillsno mess12 min

Tuck fabric cloths of different textures into an empty tissue box and let your baby pull them out one by one.

Setup

Remove the plastic film from the slot of an empty tissue box. Tuck three fabric cloths of different textures inside so the corners poke out.

Steps

  1. Show a corner and pull it out slowly: “Ooh, what’s in there?”
  2. Hold the box out to your baby and let them pull out the next cloth themselves.
  3. Let them touch each one and name it: “Soft”, “Fluffy”, “Smooth”.
  4. Tuck the cloths back in while your baby watches. Let them choose which one to pull first.

What you need

  • an empty tissue box
  • three fabric cloths in different textures

Safety

Instead of ribbons, use small cloths with no fringes, threads or ties.

If your baby chews the box, take it away. Stay close for the whole game.

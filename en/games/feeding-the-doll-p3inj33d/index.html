Feeding the doll

9-12 monthsemotionsno mess6 min

Feed the doll with a spoon, pass the spoon to your baby and see who they decide to feed next.

Setup

Sit a doll or soft toy in front of your baby and put a baby spoon next to it.

Steps

  1. Bring the spoon to the doll’s mouth: “Yum. Dolly is eating.”
  2. Hold the spoon out to your baby and wait to see if they take it. If not, feed the doll again yourself.
  3. When your baby brings the spoon to the doll, say “Yum” together. If they’re reaching, gently support the spoon from below.
  4. Offer your own mouth: “What about me?” Let your baby decide who to feed next: the doll or you.

What you need

  • a doll or soft toy
  • a baby spoon

Safety

Use a large baby spoon and a toy with no small parts that could come off.

Your baby plays with the spoon only while sitting: no crawling or walking with it in their mouth.

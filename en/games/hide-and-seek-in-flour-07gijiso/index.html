Hide and seek in flour

15-18 monthsattention14 min

Under a thin layer of flour on a tray are pictures you drew, and your child clears them away with a finger and palm.

Steps

  1. Set the tray in front of your child: “What's hiding in there?” Trace a finger path to the edge of a picture.

What you need

  • a rimmed tray
  • a sheet of paper
  • a marker
  • 3 tablespoons of flour or cornstarch
  • a wet towel

Safety

For a wheat allergy, use cornstarch instead; keep the bag to yourself and pour it low, without raising dust.

If your child puts flour in their mouth or rubs their eyes, wipe their hands and end the game.

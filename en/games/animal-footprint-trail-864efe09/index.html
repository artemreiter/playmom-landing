Animal footprint trail

2-4 yearsmovementno mess25 min

Walk along paper footprints on the floor and change the way you move: hop like a rabbit and stomp like a bear.

Setup

Draw simple footprints on six sheets of paper: circles for a rabbit, big ovals for a bear. Lay the sheets in a path on a flat floor and tape down the edges.

Steps

  1. Show the first footprint and how the animal moves: the rabbit hops, the bear stomps and sways.
  2. Walk the trail together, changing the way you move on each sheet.
  3. Let your child pick the animal for the next round, and you copy them.
  4. Swap: you walk like an animal, and your child guesses which one it is.

What you need

  • six sheets of paper
  • a felt-tip pen
  • masking tape

Safety

Tape the sheets to the floor so they don’t slip, and keep the trail on a flat floor with no steps.

Keep the trail short and in one room: no running and no crawling under furniture.

Muffin tray snack

12-15 monthsindependence37 min

Put familiar soft foods in the cups of a muffin tray and see what your baby picks first and offers to you.

Setup

Put three or four soft foods your baby already eats in the cups of a silicone muffin tray, such as banana and cooked carrot, cut into strips the size of an adult’s finger.

Steps

  1. Sit your baby in the high chair, put the tray in front of them and see which cup they choose first.
  2. Name what your baby picks up: “Banana. Soft and sweet.”
  3. When a cup is empty, put a different food in it and see if your baby notices.
  4. Suggest moving a piece to another cup or offering one to you.

What you need

  • a silicone muffin tray
  • 3-4 soft foods your baby already eats
  • a bib

Safety

Your baby eats only sitting up, with you beside them watching the whole time.

Only familiar soft foods: no nuts, whole peas, grapes or hard raw vegetables.

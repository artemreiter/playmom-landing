Sticky treasure board

18-30 monthsfine motor skillsno mess19 min

Stick large pieces of paper and fabric onto tape, and your baby peels them off and puts them in a treasure chest box.

Steps

  1. Peel off one piece while your baby watches and put it in the box: “Treasure in the chest.”

What you need

  • wide packing tape
  • a chopping board
  • 6 pieces of paper and fabric
  • a box

Safety

Only pieces bigger than a palm: no pompoms, feathers or sweet wrappers.

If the tape comes unstuck or your baby puts it in their mouth, take it away.

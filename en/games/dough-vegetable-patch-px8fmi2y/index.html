Dough vegetable patch

24-30 monthsfine motor skills22 min

Shape big carrots and tomatoes from play dough together, plant them on a plate patch and bring in the harvest.

Steps

  1. Roll a carrot the length of your palm, press on a green top and plant it in the patch.

What you need

  • play dough in three colors with a suitable age label
  • a big plate
  • a bowl

Safety

Use play dough with an age label, keep the vegetables big, no toothpicks or beads.

If your child puts the dough in their mouth, take it away and end the game.

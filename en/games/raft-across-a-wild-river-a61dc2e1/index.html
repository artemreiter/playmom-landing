Raft across a wild river

30-36 monthsgross motor skillsno mess17 min

A blue blanket becomes a river: your child ferries toys across in a box raft, then rides in it while you make the waves.

Setup

Spread a blue blanket or sheet on the carpet: this is the river. Put a large cardboard box with no staples at the edge: this is the raft.

Steps

  1. Let your child choose 2-3 toy passengers and sit them on the raft.
  2. Your child pushes the raft ahead of them across the river to the other bank. Greet the passengers: “Here we are.”
  3. Be a wave: say “Shhh” and gently rock the box with your hand while your child holds the passengers steady.
  4. Now your child sits in the raft and you slowly push them to the bank. Then your child decides who to carry next.

What you need

  • a blue blanket or sheet
  • a large cardboard box
  • 2-3 soft toys

Safety

Push the box slowly, away from furniture corners; no rope is needed.

The box has no staples or sharp edges, and your child sits in it rather than standing.

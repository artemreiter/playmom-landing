Spoon catapult

4-6 yearsattentionno mess30 min

Build a catapult from a spoon and a cardboard tube, then take turns launching paper balls into cups worth different points.

Setup

Tape a paper towel tube to the floor and rest a plastic spoon across it, scoop down. Set 3 cups farther away, marked 1, 3 and 5 points.

Steps

  1. Roll up a paper ball, set it in the scoop, and press the handle hard: the ball flies.
  2. Let your child fire it themselves and see how far it goes when they press harder.
  3. Let your child slide the spoon along the tube and see how that changes the flight.
  4. After 10 balls, count the points on the cups, then you take a turn while your child counts your score.

What you need

  • a plastic spoon
  • a paper towel tube
  • masking tape
  • paper for the balls
  • 3 plastic cups
  • a marker

Safety

No rubber bands needed, use only soft paper for the balls.

Fire only at the cups, never at people.

If there are children under 3 nearby, pick up the paper balls afterward.

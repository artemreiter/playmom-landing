Cushion obstacle course

18-21 monthsgross motor skillsno mess19 min

Work through a course of cushions and a blanket together: your baby crawls under a bridge made from your arms and legs, then rearranges the course.

Steps

  1. Go through the course first: climb over the cushion, crawl across the blanket, and step over the last one.

What you need

  • 2 sofa cushions
  • a blanket

Safety

Don’t use chairs or a blanket tunnel: keep everything on the floor, away from furniture corners.

Hold the bridge position steadily and never lower yourself onto your baby; keep the cushions on the rug only.

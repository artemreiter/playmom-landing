Finger ice rink

18-21 monthssenses15 min

Your baby runs their fingers over ice in a container, leaves melting marks and slides a rubber toy across the rink.

Setup

The day before, freeze a finger-deep layer of water in a flat container. Stand it on a towel and keep a mug of warm water by you.

Steps

  1. Run your finger over the ice: “Cold. Look, a mark.” Let your baby touch it too.
  2. Show them a handprint. Let your baby choose where to leave theirs.
  3. Put a rubber toy on the ice and give it a push: “Off it goes.” Let your baby slide it themselves.
  4. Pour warm water wherever your baby points and watch little dips appear.

What you need

  • a flat plastic container
  • water
  • a towel
  • a rubber toy bigger than your baby’s fist
  • a mug

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach; the water in the mug is bath warm.

Leave the ice in the container and clear away any chips; the toy is bigger than your baby’s fist. If their hands go red, dry and warm them.

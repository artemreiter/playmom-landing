Houseplant keeper

4-7 yearsemotions17 min

Your child takes charge of a plant: checks the soil, waters it, measures its growth and draws it in a journal.

Steps

  1. Invite your child to become the plant’s keeper: they decide when to water it.

What you need

  • a houseplant or an onion
  • a small watering can
  • a notebook
  • a ruler
  • coloured pencils

Safety

Make sure your child doesn’t put soil or leaves in their mouth, and wash hands after looking after the plant.

Water only while you’re there, and wipe up spills straight away.

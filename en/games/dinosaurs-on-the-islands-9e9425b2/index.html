Dinosaurs on the islands

3-4 yearsgross motor skillsno mess20 min

Make your way across islands of sofa cushions and rescue toy dinosaurs from the swamp on the floor.

Steps

  1. Say: “The floor has turned into a swamp, the dinosaurs are in trouble,” and step onto the first island.

Open in the app

What you need

  • 5 sofa cushions
  • 3 toy dinosaurs or other figures
  • a rug

Safety

The cushions lie on the rug with no blanket underneath, a step apart, there’s no need to jump.

If a cushion shifts, straighten it before the next move.

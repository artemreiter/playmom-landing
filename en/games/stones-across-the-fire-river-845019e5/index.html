Stones across the fire river

2-5 yearsmovementno mess15 min

The floor is a river of fire: your child crosses on cushion stones, brings back the treasure and lays out stones for you.

Setup

Lay five cushions on the rug, a child’s step apart, and put a treasure toy on the far bank.

Steps

  1. Say: “The floor is a river of fire. We only walk on the stones.” Go first yourself.
  2. Your child crosses the river, stepping or jumping from stone to stone. Hold out your hand if they ask.
  3. If your child steps on the floor, say “Hot”: they go back to the stone before.
  4. On the far bank, your child picks up the treasure and carries it back.
  5. Swap: your child lays out the stones their own way, and you cross.

What you need

  • 5 cushions
  • a rug
  • a toy

Safety

Cushions on a rug only: on a smooth floor they slide out from under your feet.

No more than a child’s step between stones; no climbing on furniture.

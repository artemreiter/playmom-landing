Chase the soft ball

6-9 monthsgross motor skillsno mess14 min

Roll a big soft ball to your baby on a mat, let them push and pat it, and roll it back each time.

Steps

  1. Slowly roll the ball to your baby’s hands: “Here comes the ball.” Wait for your baby to pat or push it.

What you need

  • a big soft ball
  • a blanket or play mat

Safety

Use a soft ball bigger than your fist, with no stickers or small parts.

Stay close while your baby is on their tummy, and roll them over when they get tired.

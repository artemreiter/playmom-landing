A road for the toy car

15-18 monthsthinkingno mess15 min

Stick a masking tape road on the floor, drive a toy car along it and add a turn wherever your baby points.

Steps

  1. Drive the car along the tape: “Beep beep, off we go.” Stop at the toy: “We’re here.”

What you need

  • masking tape
  • a large toy car

Safety

Only you stick the tape, and only on the floor; peel it off after playing so your baby can’t chew the scraps.

The car is bigger than your baby’s fist, with no wheels or small parts that could come off.

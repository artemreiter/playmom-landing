Cardboard box tunnel

9-12 monthsgross motor skillsno mess17 min

Lay a box that’s open at both ends on the floor, call your baby from the other end and greet them with a hug.

Setup

Open the flaps at both ends of a large cardboard box and lay it on the floor. You can add a second box and tape them together on the outside.

Steps

  1. Sit your baby at one end and yourself at the other. Peek inside: “Peekaboo, I’m here.”
  2. Show a favourite toy at your end and call your baby by name.
  3. When your baby crawls through, hug them: “You made it.” Hand them the toy.
  4. Swap ends and do it again. Let your baby choose which end to crawl from.

What you need

  • 1 or 2 large cardboard boxes
  • sticky tape
  • a favourite toy

Safety

Use boxes with no staples, and stick tape only on the outside.

The tunnel lies on the floor, open at both ends, with nothing on top.

Treasure in the basket

12-15 monthsattentionno mess22 min

Hide big toys in a basket under scarves and let your baby pull off the fabric to find the treasure.

Steps

  1. Shake the basket: “Someone’s hiding in there.”

What you need

  • a basket or box
  • three large toys
  • two scarves or tea towels

Safety

Use toys bigger than your baby’s fist, with no small parts; no rice, grains or small objects.

Don’t cover your baby’s face with a scarf, and put the scarves away after playing.

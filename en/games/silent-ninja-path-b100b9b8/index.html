Silent ninja path

3-4 yearsgross motor skillsno mess23 min

Build a path on the rug from cushions, a towel and strips of tape for your child to cross like a ninja without a sound.

Setup

Lay 2 cushion-rocks and a towel-bridge on the rug, and stick down 2 strips of masking tape as lasers. The path leads to the dining table.

Steps

  1. Say: “You're a ninja. Cross the path without a sound.” Show the first stretch yourself, slowly.
  2. Your child steps over the rocks, walks the bridge, steps over the lasers and crawls under the table.
  3. If a sound is made or something moves, your child quietly starts that stretch again.
  4. Swap: your child rearranges the obstacles while you cross the path as they listen.

What you need

  • 2 cushions
  • a towel
  • masking tape
  • a dining table

Safety

Use only tape strips on the floor for lasers, no string, rubber bands or ribbons.

Crawl only under a stable table, never under chairs.

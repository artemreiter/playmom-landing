Rescue the toys from the ice

12-15 monthssenses22 min

Pour warm water over a block of ice together and free the big toys frozen inside it.

Steps

  1. Let your baby touch the ice: “It’s cold. The duck is stuck.”

What you need

  • a plastic bowl
  • two large bath toys
  • a tray with raised edges
  • a plastic cup
  • warm water
  • a towel

Safety

Stay within arm’s reach and keep the water on the tray shallow. The water should be bath warm; test it on your wrist.

The toys are bigger than your baby’s fist; clear away any chips of ice straight away.

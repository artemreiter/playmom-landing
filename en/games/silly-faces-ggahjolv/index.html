Silly faces

15-18 monthsemotionsno prepno mess4 min

Pull silly faces for your baby, copy their expressions and let them choose which face to see again.

Setup

No preparation needed. Sit face to face with your baby.

Steps

  1. Puff out your cheeks and slowly let the air out: “Pfff.”
  2. Wait for a smile or a copy, then show another face: raise your eyebrows or poke out the tip of your tongue.
  3. Say: “Your turn,” and copy any face your baby makes, even a simple smile.
  4. Let your baby choose what to show again: cheeks or tongue. Take turns.

Safety

If your baby looks scared or frowns, smile straight away and end the game.

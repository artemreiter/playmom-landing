Ramp for the ball

24-30 monthsthinkingno mess16 min

Build a sloping ramp out of books and a board, roll a ball down it and knock over cups at the bottom.

Steps

  1. Put the ball at the top of the ramp and let go: “Off it rolls.”

What you need

  • 3 thick books
  • a chopping board
  • a ball bigger than your baby’s fist
  • 3 plastic cups

Safety

The ball is bigger than your baby’s fist, don’t use buttons, beads or small balls.

Keep the stack of books below your baby’s knee, and nobody stands on the ramp.

Planting beans with tweezers

4-5 yearsfine motor skills14 min

Your child uses tweezers to plant beans and peas into the cells of an ice cube tray like garden rows, then harvests them again.

Steps

  1. Pick up one bean with the tweezers and place it in a cell: “First row planted.”

What you need

  • an ice cube tray
  • plastic tweezers
  • large dried beans
  • dried peas
  • a tray
  • a small jar

Safety

Watch that the beans don’t end up in the mouth, nose or ears, and put them away if younger children are nearby.

Use plastic tweezers with rounded tips only.

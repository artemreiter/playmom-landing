Crawl for the scarf

6-9 monthsmovementno mess15 min

Trail a large scarf along the floor in front of your baby while they reach, crawl to it and pull it out of your hand.

Steps

  1. Slowly trail a scarf along the floor in front of your baby so the edge rustles near their hands.

What you need

  • 2 large light scarves or tea towels

Safety

Hold the scarves yourself: don’t tie them to chairs and don’t use ribbons or string.

Don’t drape a scarf over your baby’s face or head, and stay close the whole time.

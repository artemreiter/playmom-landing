A drawing for two

12-15 monthsemotions13 min

Draw with wax crayons on one big sheet: you copy your baby’s scribbles, and they choose the colours.

Setup

Tape a large sheet of paper to the floor or table with masking tape at the corners. Put two chunky wax crayons beside it.

Steps

  1. Draw a long line with a crayon: “Whoosh, a path.”
  2. Give your baby the second crayon: let them leave their mark anywhere.
  3. Copy their movement next to it: dots if they tap, lines if they sweep. Name it: “Dot, dot.”
  4. Hold out two crayons in different colours: let your baby pick one and give the other to you.
  5. Hang the drawing on the wall at your baby’s eye level: “You and I drew this.”

What you need

  • a large sheet of paper
  • two chunky wax crayons
  • masking tape

Safety

Use chunky wax crayons labelled for your baby’s age; if your baby puts one in their mouth, take it away and stop.

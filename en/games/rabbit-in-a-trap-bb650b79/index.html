Rabbit in a trap

4-5 yearsindependenceno mess27 min

As a secret agent, your child crosses a course of tape, a duvet and a towel to rescue a rabbit from under a chair.

Setup

Sit the toy under a chair. On the way to it, stick a strip of masking tape on the floor and lay down a folded duvet and a towel.

Steps

  1. Explain: the rabbit is trapped under the chair, and only a secret agent can rescue it.
  2. The agent steps over the tape laser, crawls over the duvet mountain and jumps across the towel river.
  3. No reaching for the rabbit by hand: let your child choose the ladle or the towel and pull the toy out.
  4. Swap: your child hides the rabbit and changes the course, and you become the agent.

What you need

  • a soft toy
  • a chair
  • masking tape
  • a duvet
  • a towel
  • a ladle

Safety

The laser is only stuck to the floor: don’t stretch string, yarn or ribbons across.

The chair stays in place, nobody climbs on it, and the rabbit is reached from the floor.

Caterpillar hunts for treasure

24-30 monthsgross motor skillsno mess19 min

Crawl together along a row of sofa cushions and hunt for soft toys hidden between them.

Steps

  1. Get down on all fours and crawl over the cushions first: “I’m a caterpillar, looking for treasure.”

Open in the app

What you need

  • 4 sofa cushions
  • 3 soft toys
  • a basket

Safety

Keep the cushions flat on the rug, with no chairs, tables or blankets on top, so nothing can fall on your baby.

Don’t hide blocks or toys with small parts.

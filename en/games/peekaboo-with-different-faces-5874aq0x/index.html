Peekaboo with different faces

12-15 monthsemotionsno prepno mess4 min

Hide your face behind your hands and pop up sometimes smiling, sometimes surprised, then let your baby hide too.

Setup

Nothing to get ready. Sit facing your baby at arm’s length.

Steps

  1. Cover your face with your hands and ask: “Where am I?”
  2. Reveal your face with a big smile, say “Peekaboo” and give your baby time to answer with laughter or movement.
  3. Next time appear with a surprised face: eyebrows up, mouth in an “o” shape.
  4. Let your baby cover their eyes with their own hands, and happily find them: “There you are.”
  5. Repeat whichever face your baby answered with a smile.

Safety

Only cover your own face, don’t drape anything over your baby and don’t hold their hands still.

If your baby frowns or turns away, smile and end the game.

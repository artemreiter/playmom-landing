Secret lemon message

4-5 yearsthinking24 min

Draw secret messages in lemon juice, swap them, and reveal the drawings with a warm iron.

Setup

Squeeze the juice of half a lemon into a saucer, and put cotton buds and two sheets of paper beside it.

Steps

  1. While your child isn’t looking, draw a sun on your own sheet with a cotton bud dipped in juice.
  2. Let your child draw a secret message on their sheet: a house, a star or a pattern.
  3. After 5 minutes, once the sheets have dried, swap them and guess what’s hidden.
  4. Lay the sheet on a folded towel and press it with a warm iron until the drawing appears.

What you need

  • a lemon
  • cotton buds
  • white paper
  • a saucer
  • an iron
  • a towel

Safety

Only you hold the iron, your child sits on the other side of the table, and you put the iron away to cool once the game is over.

If your child is allergic to citrus, draw with milk instead, and rinse juice out of an eye with water.

Blanket wave dance

21-24 monthsgross motor skillsno mess10 min

Raise and lower a blanket together with your baby to make waves, and float light soft toys on top of them.

Setup

Spread a blanket on the floor and set three light soft toys beside it.

Steps

  1. Take hold of the blanket from two sides: you on one edge, your baby on the other.
  2. Slowly raise and lower it, crouching down and stretching up together: these are the waves.
  3. Let your baby choose a toy and set it in the middle: “Off it sails.”
  4. Make a bigger wave so the toy bounces, and let your baby decide who sails next.

What you need

  • a big blanket or sheet
  • three light soft toys

Safety

The blanket never covers your baby’s face or head, and there’s no need to hide underneath it.

Play on the rug, with nothing underfoot.

Kitchen drum kit

9-12 monthsgross motor skillsno mess10 min

Tap wooden spoons on upturned saucepans and a bowl, and your baby answers with their own beats.

Steps

  1. Tap the big pan with your spoon, “Boom,” then the small one, “Ting.”

What you need

  • 2 saucepans of different sizes
  • a plastic bowl
  • 2 wooden spoons
  • a towel

Safety

Only cold, empty pans without lids: lids pinch fingers.

Tap gently and not right next to your baby’s ear.

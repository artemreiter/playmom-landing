Islands over the lava

4-5 yearsgross motor skillsno mess18 min

Your child jumps across islands made of towels and mats without stepping into the lava, and follows the tasks you set.

Steps

  1. Announce that the floor has turned to lava, and jump from island to island first yourself.

Open in the app

What you need

  • 5 towels or small mats
  • a rug

Safety

Use flat islands only, and only on the rug, since feet slip on cushions and paper.

Place the islands so your child can land without a run-up, well away from furniture with sharp corners.

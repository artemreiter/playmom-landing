Guess the shadow

24-30 monthsthinkingno mess11 min

Make a bunny or dog shadow with one hand and let your child guess, then they show their own hand in the beam.

Setup

Dim the lights so you can still see the floor. Take a torch with a screwed-down battery cover and shine it on a light-coloured wall.

Steps

  1. With your free hand, make a bunny: two fingers up. Let the bunny hop along the wall.
  2. Make a dog: hand on its side, thumb up. Ask: “Who’s this, the bunny or the dog?”
  3. Let your child put a hand in the beam: open palm, fist, a wave. Say what the shadow looks like.
  4. Ask which shadow to show again, and make up together where it will run on the wall.

What you need

  • a torch with a screwed-down battery cover
  • a light-coloured wall

Safety

Keep the torch in your hand the whole time and shine it at the wall, not in anyone’s eyes.

If your child feels uneasy in the half-dark, turn the light on and stop.

Letters from fantasy land

3-4 yearsspeechno messbedtime22 min

Lay out foil, fabric and a sweet wrapper as letters from a land of fantasy, then let your child “read” them with a finger and make up stories.

Setup

Put a big piece of foil, a scrap of fabric, a large sweet wrapper and a piece of wallpaper on the table: these are letters from a land of fantasy.

Steps

  1. Let your child choose a letter, and ask: “Who sent this one?”
  2. Your child “reads” it by tracing a finger over it, while you help out if needed: foil from aliens, fabric from elves.
  3. Ask them to read the letter in the character’s voice: high-pitched, deep, or a whisper.
  4. Swap roles: you “read” a new letter, and your child decides who it’s from.

What you need

  • a big piece of foil
  • a scrap of fabric
  • a large sweet wrapper
  • a piece of old wallpaper

Safety

Don’t glue on beads or buttons, draw any secret symbols instead.

If there are children under 3 nearby, pick up any scraps of foil.

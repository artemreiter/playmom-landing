Towers from surprise objects

30-36 monthsindependenceno mess20 min

Build towers together out of tubes, paper cups and yogurt tubs, and let your child decide what goes on top.

Setup

Lay out 5 toilet paper tubes, 5 paper cups and 3 clean yogurt tubs on the floor.

Steps

  1. Stand a tube on a cup and invite your child to add anything on top.
  2. Let your child choose what to stack next, and try out different combinations.
  3. When the tower falls, don’t rush to help, just ask: “What should go at the bottom to keep it standing?”
  4. Swap roles: you build to your child’s instructions, and they decide when to knock the tower down.

What you need

  • 5 toilet paper tubes
  • 5 paper cups
  • 3 yogurt tubs

Safety

Use only big, lightweight items with no lids, sponges or small parts needed.

If your child chews a cup or tube, take away the soggy piece.

Giant’s web on the floor

30-36 monthsgross motor skillsno mess22 min

Cross the giant’s web of tape strips on the floor: step over each thread and crawl through the gate.

Setup

Stick 5 strips of tape on the floor in a zigzag across the room: these are the threads the giant left behind.

Steps

  1. Step over the first thread with a high step and whisper: “Quiet, don’t touch it.”
  2. Let your child cross the whole web, stepping over the strips without touching them.
  3. Stand with your legs wide apart: this is the giant’s gate, and let your child crawl underneath.
  4. Now your child leads: let them decide how to cross the web, sideways or crawling, and you copy.

What you need

  • masking tape

Safety

The threads are only tape on the floor: no string, rope or ribbon stretched between furniture.

Play barefoot or in non-slip socks on a rug, and don’t rush your child.

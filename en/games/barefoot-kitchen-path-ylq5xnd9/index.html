Barefoot kitchen path

15-18 monthsgross motor skillsno mess15 min

Walk barefoot together along a path made from a baking tray, a chopping board, a silicone mat and a towel, naming what your baby’s feet feel.

Steps

  1. Take off your baby’s socks, hold their hand, and step onto the tray together: “Ooh, cold.”

What you need

  • a flat baking tray
  • a wooden chopping board
  • a silicone mat
  • a towel

Safety

Keep the path on the rug, and hold your baby’s hand the whole time.

Leave out wire racks and muffin tins, since little fingers can get stuck in them, and make sure the tray has no sharp edges.

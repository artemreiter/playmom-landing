Delete your PlayMom account and data

PlayMom respects your right to delete your account and all data we hold about you.

In-app deletion (preferred)

  1. Open the PlayMom app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile avatar (top-right of the Home screen).
  3. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted within 30 days.

Email deletion request

If you can't access the app or prefer email, send a deletion request to:

artem@reiterweb.com

Include the email address you used to register. We respond within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Account email and profile data
  • Child profiles, activity history, favorites
  • Purchase history (where legally permitted)
  • Authentication tokens and app interaction logs

What we keep

  • Anonymized analytics that cannot be tied to your identity (for product improvement)
  • Transaction records required by tax law (Polish VAT) for the legally required retention period

See full Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how we handle your data.
